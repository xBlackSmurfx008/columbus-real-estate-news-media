## Lead 1 — One Twenty Vine: 242-Unit Apartment Proposed for Arena District Parking Lot

**Summary:** The Columbus Navigator reported on September 24, 2026 that the Downtown Commission approved plans for One Twenty Vine, a seven-story, 242-unit apartment complex at 120 Vine St. in Columbus's Arena District. The project, a partnership between HP Land Development LTD (a Hamilton Parker affiliate) and Nationwide Realty Investors, would replace a large gravel surface parking lot currently used for paid public parking by Blue Jackets fans and North Market visitors. The commission approval occurred on September 22, 2026. The design features two seven-story wings joined by an enclosed bridge, ground-floor resident amenities, fully enclosed first-floor parking with rear entry, an internal courtyard with a pool, and a skywalk connecting to the adjacent Kilbourne Garage. The site consolidates nine parcels and adjacent right-of-way. No construction timeline has been announced; building permits are the next step.

**Key context:**
- The Arena District is already home to Two Twenty Vine (220 Vine St.), another Nationwide Realty Investors apartment development along the same corridor, making this a second major NRI residential play on Vine Street.
- The project would remove one of the more accessible public surface parking lots near Nationwide Arena, raising potential questions about event-night parking capacity and turnover.
- The architect is The Columbus Architectural Studio.

## What worked / what stalled

**Worked: the newsroom kept producing, and quality discipline went up.** Stories were drafted and staged every day from Aug 5 through Aug 13. The new gate added claim traceability, SEO metadata checks, and a scoring rubric. Production and verification are not the constraint.

**Stalled: the site went dark for readers.** The snapshot says 1 article published in the window. The newsroom staged 13 in the same period. Visitors have seen one new story in twelve days. The gate has an editor-shaped hole in it: review is a human step, no human is doing it, and 14 of 17 drafts are missing the hero the gate requires. Every downstream number (subscribers +0, leads 0, affiliate clicks 0) is meaningless while the shelf is empty. A daily paper that publishes monthly is not a daily paper.

**Stalled: we still have no ears.** Leads 0, members 0, subscribers stuck at 1. With no pageview data we cannot tell a traffic problem from a conversion problem, so we cannot say which of the four funnels (FSBO, investor-sale, capital, renter) is weakest. Per the owner's rule, empty tables mean awareness and instrumentation come before conversion work. Principle 17 says be quiet and listen. We have nothing to listen with. Instrumentation has now been directed three times without approval.

**Stalled: the approval loop, in two places.** Directive approval: nothing directed on Jul 17, Aug 2, or Aug 3 was approved, so nothing was built. Editorial approval: the new gate depends on a daily human review that is not happening. Both bottlenecks are the same person. The fixes below make the editorial review fast and unmissable; only the owner can make it happen.

## Directives (prioritized)

### P1 — Make the review queue clearable: heroes on every draft, a daily review digest, one-click approval (new this week)
- Why: Principle 21 (a killer offer won't save you in a dead market) and principle 20 (strong marketing beats a strong product with no message). KPI gap: 1 article live in the window against 13 staged. The gate is good policy; keep it. But a gate nobody staffs is a wall. The CTO's job is to make review take five minutes a day.
- Definition of done: Every pending draft reaches reviewable state: the illustration job (or a fallback) attaches a hero to the 14 drafts missing one, and machine rubric pre-checks are displayed per draft. The admin panel gets a review queue view: pending drafts, pre-check results, rendered preview, and a single approve action that flips `status='live'`. A daily review-queue digest (count, titles, direct links) reaches the owner's inbox whenever the queue is non-empty. Owner action flagged: review the 17-draft backlog. Success metric: next Monday's KPI shows articles published in window matching articles staged in window.

### P2 — Instrument server-side traffic and funnel visibility (fourth issue; first directed 2026-07-17)
- Why: Principles 5 (ignore psychology and you market blind) and 17 (be quiet and listen). KPI gap: subscribers, members, leads, and affiliate clicks all read 0 with no pageview data to say why. This directive is unchanged from the last three reports because the gap is unchanged. It would also have surfaced the empty-shelf problem within a day instead of eleven.
- Definition of done: A pageview endpoint and table persist views server-side, privacy-friendly, no popups or consent theater. The tracker mounts on `blog/[slug]` and all four funnel pages (`/sell/your-home`, `/sell/investment-property`, `/invest/deploy-capital`, `/rent/find-a-home`). `kpi-report.mjs` gains a traffic section: pageviews, top articles, per-funnel-page views, view-to-lead rate. Next Monday's snapshot shows non-null traffic numbers.

### P3 — Restart distribution behind the gate: social copy on every approval day (carried from 2026-08-03 P2, reframed)
- Why: Principle 20 and principle 26 (good visuals sell more than you think). Gaps: subscribers stuck at 1 while 13 stories were staged; social channel copy last written 2026-06-04. The new workflow already says social copy follows editor approval. Nothing has been approved, so nothing has shipped anywhere since June. Once P1 opens the gate, every approved story needs a push the same day.
- Definition of done: On every day at least one draft is approved to live, the pipeline writes `content/<channel>/<date>.md` distribution copy for the approved stories, for 7 consecutive approval days. Approved articles ship with their hero attached (covered by P1) so the social copy has a visual. Subscriber count moves off 1 within two weeks of P1 and P3 both running.

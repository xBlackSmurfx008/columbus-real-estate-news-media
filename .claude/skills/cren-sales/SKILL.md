# CREN Sales Principles

The owner's sales principles bank. Every funnel, CTA, offer, and growth directive on
columbusrealestatenews.com is evaluated against these. Tagline: "We will not get left
behind. We will build."

## Voice rules (always apply)
- Short declarative sentences.
- No em dashes in body copy.
- Outcomes-first framing.
- Combine with `.claude/skills/cren-copywriting` (4th-grade reading level) for all public copy.

## The 26 Principles

### Psychology & Positioning
1. People purchase with emotions, then justify with logic.
2. You're not offering a product. You're offering a transformation.
3. State your price, then stay silent. Let silence work for you.
4. Selling relief is stronger than selling dreams.
5. If you ignore psychology, you're marketing blind.

### Offers & Trust
6. A solid guarantee doubles sales. Refunds aren't the enemy.
7. Strong copy isn't clever, it's deeply researched.
8. Don't force demand. Find it and position yourself.
9. No one cares about your product. They care what it does for them.
10. Add real faces to testimonials. It creates instant trust.

### Value & Pricing
11. You don't need fancy tools. Focus matters most.
12. Be valuable first, then rare. That's how wealth grows.
13. The market isn't saturated. Your offer just isn't landing.
14. Price isn't the issue. Perceived value is.
15. Specificity isn't optional. It's required.

### Customers & Copy
16. A loyal customer is worth more than any funnel.
17. Want more sales? Be quiet and listen.
18. Use "you" and "your" in your copy. Keep it about them.
19. Hook quickly. Keep them curious always.
20. Strong marketing beats a strong product with no message.

### Closing & Longevity
21. A killer offer won't save you in a dead market.
22. Handle objections upfront. Answer them in your FAQ.
23. Don't sacrifice long-term trust for quick cash.
24. Your story is your most powerful sales weapon.
25. Irresistible offers = proof + urgency + bonuses + risk reversal.
26. Good visuals sell more than you think. Don't ignore them.

## How to apply on this property
- Every funnel page and CTA must pass 9, 18, 19 (about them, "you" language, fast hook).
- Offers lean on 25 — but only with HONEST proof. Never invent testimonials, stats, or urgency (23 outranks 25).
- Free→paid conversion must not be annoying: upgrade prompts appear only after a
  conversion moment (post-signup, post-lead), never as popups or paywalls.
- The capital-deployment funnel never promises returns and always carries the
  educational/not-an-offer-of-securities line.
- FTC disclosure appears above every affiliate block.

## Business model (context for CMO/CSO directives)
We are real estate investors and lead generators. Revenue order: lead intake
(FSBO sellers, investors selling, capital partners, renters) → affiliate links →
minimal display ads. No paywall for most content. Free membership now; paid tier
later (premium data + deal alerts + investor community). Full Columbus metro first,
then hyperlocal neighborhood hubs.

## Lead 1 — Worthington Referendum Opponents Emerge as Validation Deadline Approaches

**Summary:** One month after Democracy Before Developers submitted ~3,976 petition signatures to force a 2027 ballot referendum on the Worthington City Council's July rezoning of 445 E. Dublin-Granville Road for a 246-unit apartment complex, pro-development Worthington residents have publicly pushed back, warning the referendum could harm future development prospects in the city. The Franklin County Board of Elections is expected to complete signature validation by mid-October 2026.

**Key details:**
- Referendum target: City Council's July 20, 2026 rezoning of ~20 acres at 445 E. Dublin-Granville Rd. (formerly the Harding Hospital site, owned by disability nonprofit I Am Boundless) for a $52.4 million, 248-unit housing development.
- Petitioners: Democracy Before Developers, submitting 3,976 signatures September 21, 2026 (threshold: 3,017). Organizer Marty Shumway estimated 85–90% of signatures valid.
- New development: Pro-development residents now publicly opposing the referendum, expressing concern it could chill future development approvals and investment in Worthington.
- Context: City Council's July 2026 vote was 5-0, reflecting a council with four members elected in 2025 on pro-development platforms.
- The I Am Boundless nonprofit intends to use proceeds from the land sale to expand its workforce by 65–80 jobs and fund an autism center.
- If signatures are certified, the referendum would appear on the November 2027 ballot.
- Mid-October 2026: Expected Board of Elections certification decision — an imminent milestone.

**Sentiment:** Mixed. Referendum supporters characterize it as a density and tax-abatement concern, not opposition to development in principle. Pro-development opponents of the referendum counter that the effort could dampen development interest in Worthington.

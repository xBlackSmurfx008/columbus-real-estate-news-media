### Central Ohio April 2026: Sales Down 13.6%, Inventory Climbs 13.4%, Prices Up 8.3%

- **What**: Columbus Realtors' April 2026 report (published May 14 by WOSU) shows 2,202 closed sales — down 13.6% year-over-year — while active inventory rose 13.4% to 5,027 homes. The median sales price climbed 8.3% year-over-year to $346,500. Days on market expanded from 32 to 39. Franklin County closings fell 15.4%; Delaware County held at 254 closings with a $539,000 median.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: The Central Ohio market is bifurcating — volume is softening as higher rates and affordability strain slow transactions, yet prices remain elevated and inventory is building for the first time since 2022.

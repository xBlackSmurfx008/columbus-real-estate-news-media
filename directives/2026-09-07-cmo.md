### P1 — Get the funnel conversion layer live on production and prove it with telemetry
- Why: KPI gap — 0 CTA clicks and 0 views on the three highest-priority
  funnels (FSBO, investor-sale, capital) across 120 pageviews, the third
  consecutive weekly snapshot with this exact reading. The fixes for this gap
  (taxonomy-matched article CTAs, CTA click telemetry, funnel disclosures,
  honest newsletter copy) were merged 09-04 and remain undeployed per the

  offer isn't landing because it isn't placed), 19 (no hook is possible with
  no placement), and 23 (the old template's false "every Tuesday" newsletter
  promise burns trust daily while it stays live). This outranks everything
  else because it gates every conversion metric on the property: no funnel
  exposure means no leads, no members, no affiliate clicks, regardless of
  copy quality or traffic growth.
- Definition of done: (a) the root cause of the stalled Vercel pipeline is
  named in writing (disconnected Git integration, failing build, or paused
  project); (b) if the integration cannot be repaired same-day, a manual
  production deploy (`vercel --prod` or dashboard redeploy) ships current
  `main` and that is recorded; (c) a live article URL fetched after deploy
  renders the taxonomy-matched contextual CTA on the article template, and no
  live page contains the "every Tuesday" copy; (d) `funnel_view` and
  `cta_click` events from production pages are being recorded — proven by at
  least one flagged test interaction appearing in the KPI report's
  instrumentation, so next week's snapshot can show nonzero rows the moment
  real readers see the CTAs.

### 1. DFW Land Acquires 245K SF Trophy Office and Retail at The Pointe at Polaris

- **What**: Dallas-based DFWLAND acquired the fully leased office and retail components of The Pointe at Polaris in Columbus's Polaris submarket from a joint venture of VanTrust Real Estate and NP Limited Partnership, in a deal announced April 29, 2026. Newmark brokered the transaction and arranged acquisition financing. The sale covered two Class A office buildings totaling 212,366 square feet and 33,071 square feet of retail. Office tenants include Bank of America, Fiserv, Power Home Remodeling, and Employers Health; retail tenants include Kitchen Social, Condado Tacos, Sweetwaters Coffee & Tea, and Club Pilates. ### 2. Lincoln Avenue Communities Breaks Ground on 321-Unit Landmark on Scioto

- **What**: Lincoln Avenue Communities (LAC) broke ground in April 2026 on Landmark on Scioto, a 321-unit affordable housing community on a 44.5-acre site in Columbus. Units are restricted to households earning up to 60% AMI, with two-, three-, and four-bedroom floor plans. Amenities include a fitness center, pool, and clubhouse. LAC will dedicate approximately 7 acres to the City of Columbus for a future public park. Leasing is expected to begin summer 2026; full completion in 2028.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: One of the largest single affordable housing groundbreakings in Central Ohio in recent years, and the 2BR–4BR unit mix directly addresses the chronic undersupply of family-sized affordable units in Columbus — relevant to LIHTC investors, affordable housing operators, and the neighborhoods along the Scioto corridor watching for amenity improvements.
- **Source(s)**: [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lincoln-avenue-communities-breaks-ground-on-new-affordable-housing-development-in-columbus-ohio-302737402.html) (canonical), [WhatNow Columbus](https://whatnow.com/columbus/real-estate/321-unit-affordable-housing-project-breaks-ground-in-columbus/)

### 3. Arcadia Development's 106-Unit Building Approved for Former WWCD Radio Site, Brewery District

- **What**: Columbus developer Arcadia Development received Historic Resources Commission approval (5-2 vote) to demolish the former WWCD radio station building at 1036 S. Front St. in the Brewery District and replace it with a six-story, 106-unit apartment building featuring a second-floor pool deck and a 51-car ground-floor garage. Final signage and lighting details were delegated to city staff. The independent station WWCD (formerly CD 101 and CD 102.5) ceased broadcast in 2024.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: The Brewery District sits between German Village and the southern edge of downtown; mid-scale multifamily infill here has been slower than surrounding neighborhoods. The 5-2 HRC vote and the community tension over demolishing the WWCD building will be a reference point for future infill approvals in historic Brewery District parcels.

### 4. AspireCOLUMBUS: CMHA's 82-Unit Downtown Mixed-Income Building Opening in 2026

- **What**: The Columbus Metropolitan Housing Authority (CMHA) and The Community Builders are on track to open AspireCOLUMBUS — a 12-story, 82-unit mixed-income building at 360 S. Third St. (former United Way of Central Ohio HQ) — in 2026. The building includes 31 Housing Choice Voucher-subsidized units at 30% AMI, 12 market-rate units (studios at $1,211/mo to 3BR at $2,280/mo), and 30,000 SF of commercial space on the first two floors. Construction began mid-2025.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: AspireCOLUMBUS is CMHA's highest-profile downtown mixed-income project — a 30%/market-rate/commercial stack in a single building. Its performance will influence how Columbus affordable housing developers approach downtown parcels for at least the next cycle.

### 5. Columbus Q1 2026 Housing Market: Inventory Up 3.1%, Median Holds at $315K–$320K

- **What**: Central Ohio residential closings in March 2026 rose 0.5% year-over-year to 2,118, while total inventory climbed 3.1% YoY to 4,067 units. The regional median sale price held in the $315,000–$320,000 range, with February 2026 at $315,000 (+3.3% YoY). German Village and Short North led price appreciation at 9–11%. Mortgage rates have risen to the high-5% range after a brief dip below 6% in late February.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: The inventory recovery — now approaching the low end of a balanced market — gives buyers more options heading into the spring season but hasn't yet broken the appreciation trend. Investors underwriting hold periods in 2026 can pencil modest appreciation (3–5%) without assuming a hot market.

## What worked / what stalled

**Worked: the newsroom kept producing through the outage.** Six article drafts
were filed to `content/blog/drafts/` between Aug 18 and Aug 24, including the
Columbus REALTORS July report (closed sales +6.3%, $350,000 median, decade-high
inventory) — the strongest SEO anchor story of the month. Production discipline
held: the Aug 22 brief published zero rather than force a weak item.

**Stalled: the storefront is dark, so every funnel reads zero by definition.**
A reader who lands on columbusrealestatenews.com today sees a masthead and no
journalism. Principle 21: a killer offer won't save you in a dead market — and a
site showing zero articles is a dead market of our own making. Principle 14:
perceived value on an empty page is zero. Until the database is restored there
is no meaningful judgment to make about FSBO vs. investor-sale vs. capital vs.
renter funnel performance; the denominator (a working site) is gone. Conversion
work is explicitly deferred: per the owner's rule, when the tables are empty,
availability and awareness come before conversion directives.

**Stalled: we still cannot hear anything.** This is the sixth consecutive report
(first directed 2026-07-17) without a usable listening loop — instrumentation
was finally built last week and then the database outage made it unreadable.
Principle 17 (be quiet and listen) remains unexecutable. The outage itself is
the proof of principle 5: we were marketing blind and didn't know the store had
closed.

## Directives (prioritized)

### P1 — Restore database service and put the articles back on the air
- Why: Principle 21 (a killer offer won't save you in a dead market) and
  principle 9 (no one cares about the product until it does something for
  them — a blank site does nothing for anyone). Observed gap: `/blog` and the
  homepage render 0 articles; the KPI script cannot connect at all.
- What the CTO does: resolve the Neon data-transfer overage (owner decision:
  upgrade the plan, or reduce transfer and wait out the quota window — note the
  quota does not reset until Sep 1, so "wait" means a week of dark storefront);
  read the Neon usage dashboard and name the top transfer consumer with a
  number before choosing the fix; then run the publish backlog so the stranded
  Aug 18–24 drafts (including the REALTORS July report and Zone In Phase 2) go
  live or are consciously spiked, and re-verify the hero audit.
- Definition of done: `kpi-report.mjs --window 7` completes without error;
  production `/blog` lists articles again, including at least the Aug 23 and
  Aug 24 stories; the root cause of the overage is written into this week's
  progress file with the actual transfer numbers; next Monday's snapshot has
  real rows in every table.

### P2 — Cache the content layer so a database outage can never blank the site again
- Why: Principle 23 (don't sacrifice long-term trust): a reader or advertiser
  who visits during an outage and sees an empty newsroom does not come back and
  does not distinguish "quota exceeded" from "abandoned site." Principle 26
  (visuals sell): our best visual is a full front page. Observed gap: today the
  site's failure mode for a DB error is an empty grid with placeholder text.
- What the CTO does: serve article lists and article pages from static/ISR
  cache (revalidate on publish), so the last-known-good content renders when
  the database is unreachable, and DB reads per pageview drop — which also
  attacks the transfer overage that caused this outage.
- Definition of done: with the database deliberately unreachable in a
  production-equivalent build, `/blog`, the homepage, and existing article
  pages still render the last published content (0 empty-state pages); measured
  Neon data transfer per day drops after ship and stays inside plan limits for
  7 consecutive days; the change is noted in the progress file with
  before/after transfer numbers.

### P3 — Make failures page a human the same hour (carried from 2026-08-17 P2, now proven urgent by a week-long silent outage)
- Why: Principle 17 (be quiet and listen — we cannot listen to a pipeline that
  fails silently) and principle 5 (ignore psychology and you market blind; ignore
  telemetry and you operate blind). Observed gap: the database went over quota
  and nobody was told; the daily routine ran 8 straight days without
  `DATABASE_URL` and nobody was told; both were discovered by humans reading
  briefs after the fact.
- What the CTO does: add a scheduled production health check (homepage renders
  ≥1 article link, `/blog` non-empty, one trivial DB query) that alerts on
  failure through the already-built Telegram path; put `DATABASE_URL` into the
  daily routine's configuration. Owner actions, flagged explicitly and blocking
  delivery: create the bot via BotFather and provide `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` +
  `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID` (second consecutive week this is the single external
  dependency), and confirm the routine config change at claude.ai/code/routines.
- Definition of done: a simulated failure (health check pointed at a broken
  target) produces a Telegram message within one scheduled cycle; the daily
  brief stops opening with "DATABASE_URL not available" for 7 consecutive days;
  the weekly KPI summary posts to Telegram.

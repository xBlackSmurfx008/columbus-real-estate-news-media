### VanTrust New Albany 300: 300,400-SF Spec Industrial Fully Leased Before June Delivery

- **What**: VanTrust Real Estate's New Albany 300 — a 300,400-square-foot speculative industrial building at 12550 Jug Street NW in the New Albany International Business Park — is fully leased ahead of its June 2026 delivery. The building features 36-foot clear heights and a 15-year 100% tax abatement. VanTrust has simultaneously started construction on New Albany 302 (its 7th speculative project in the park), a 302,400-SF building at the same site designed by RED Architecture and built by Elford Construction, targeting December 2026 delivery. It is VanTrust's 10th overall project in the park. Leasing for New Albany 300 was handled by Beau Taggart of KBC Advisors and Mike Hurd of Cushman & Wakefield; the tenant has not been publicly identified.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: Pre-delivery full lease-up on a 300,000-SF spec warehouse — followed immediately by a 7th spec groundbreaking — is the clearest market signal available to Columbus industrial operators: demand in the New Albany/Licking County corridor is absorbing supply faster than it can be built. The New Albany Company describes the park as "ideal for chip plant suppliers," citing Intel's planned semiconductor campus in adjacent Licking County as a durable long-term demand anchor. For industrial investors holding or acquiring in this corridor, the case for patient land and building positions has rarely been better supported by transaction data.
- **Source(s)**: [VanTrust Columbus Continues Strong Development and Leasing Momentum — Columbus Chamber](https://columbus.org/vantrust-columbus-continues-strong-development-and-leasing-momentum-vantrust-real-estate/) (canonical), [VanTrust to Start Seventh Speculative Project — ConnectCRE](https://www.connectcre.com/stories/vantrust-to-start-seventh-speculative-project-in-ohio/), [New Albany Warehouse Snapped Up Before It Even Opens — Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/04/new-albany-warehouse-snapped-up-before-it-even-opens-as-vantrust-races-to-build-more/), [VanTrust to Break Ground on New Albany Development Ideal for Chip Plant Suppliers — The New Albany Company](https://newalbanycompany.com/vantrust-to-break-ground-on-new-albany-development-ideal-for-chip-plant-suppliers/)

### Capital Line Stage 3: Gay Street Block-by-Block Closure Running June 2026–October 2027

- **What**: Construction on Stage 3 of the Capital Line — the final surface reconstruction phase — began in June 2026, bringing rolling vehicle closures to Gay Street between Fourth and Front streets in downtown Columbus. Each block closes for three to four months at a time for full roadway reconstruction, new paving, pedestrian landscaping, benches, and lighting. The full Gay Street redesign is expected to complete by end of 2026 or early 2027; the broader two-mile Capital Line corridor (Fourth St to COSI) completes by October 2027. Total project cost: $100 million. Downtown Columbus Inc. and the City of Columbus are leading the project. A Capital Line Business Rent Support Grant was available to first-floor Gay Street businesses with frontage from Fourth to Front; application deadline was May 7, 2026.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: Rolling closures are affecting Gay Street retail and restaurant operators right now — foot traffic and parking access shift each time a new block closes. Operators whose grant applications were submitted before May 7 should be tracking award announcements. When Stage 3 completes, the Capital Line will function as a premium outdoor amenity along Gay Street — the kind of public infrastructure that has historically repriced adjacent ground-floor rents upward within 12–24 months of opening. ### Drees Homes Enters Columbus as Its 12th Operating Market

- **What**: Drees Homes, a Cincinnati-based national homebuilder, expanded into the Columbus market in early 2026 — its 12th operating market, and third in Ohio alongside Cincinnati and Cleveland. The builder broke ground on its first Columbus model home at Alton Place in Hilliard in early March 2026, with the model (the Ash Lawn floor plan) set to open to buyers this summer. Pricing starts in the $800s. Drees plans to open three Columbus-area communities by end of 2026 under Columbus Division President Andy Arztner. The expansion is part of the company's "Centennial Strategy" targeting growth ahead of its 100th anniversary in 2028.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: Drees entering Columbus signals confidence from a national builder that the Columbus market can absorb new product at $800K+ price points — a clear read on where land sellers and spec home developers see the ceiling holding. Three communities in 2026 means Drees is acquiring or has acquired Columbus-area lots now; residential land sellers in Hilliard and northwest Columbus should be aware of a new active buyer in the market.
- **Source(s)**: [Drees Homes Enters Third Ohio Market With Columbus Expansion — Builder Online](https://www.builderonline.com/builder-100/strategy/drees-homes-enters-third-ohio-market-with-columbus-expansion) (canonical), [Drees Homes announces another expansion — NKy Tribune](https://nkytribune.com/2026/04/drees-homes-announces-another-expansion-into-the-columbus-ohio-area/)

Columbus industrial is moving faster than the buildings can be finished.

VanTrust Real Estate's New Albany 300 — a 300,400-square-foot speculative warehouse at 12550 Jug Street NW in the New Albany International Business Park — is fully leased ahead of its June 2026 delivery. The tenant hasn't been named publicly. The market signal is clear regardless.

What's driving it?

**Intel.** The New Albany Company — the master developer of the park — has described VanTrust's buildings as "ideal for chip plant suppliers." Intel's planned semiconductor campus in adjacent Licking County is the single largest economic development project in Ohio history. Even before the fab is fully online, the supply chain it's attracting is already filling warehouses.

**Columbus industrial fundamentals.** The Columbus MSA absorbed 8.8 million square feet of industrial space in 2025 — ranking among the top five U.S. markets. More than 5.2 million square feet are currently under construction. Positive absorption has outrun deliveries in several key submarkets, including New Albany.

**The 15-year abatement.** New Albany International Business Park's 100% tax abatement — running 15 years on qualifying improvements — remains one of the most competitive incentive structures in the Ohio industrial market. It compresses the return hurdle for spec developers and makes the park consistently attractive to first-time entrants.

**The spec cycle as a leading indicator.** When a developer leases a building before it delivers and immediately starts the next one, they're not speculating — they're trailing confirmed demand. VanTrust's pattern in New Albany is one of the cleanest representations of this dynamic anywhere in central Ohio right now.

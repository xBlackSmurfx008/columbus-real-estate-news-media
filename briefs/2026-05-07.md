### 1. Poindexter Village African American Museum Breaks Ground in King-Lincoln

- **What**: Ohio History Connection broke ground on May 6, 2026 at 290 N. Champion Ave. in the King-Lincoln Bronzeville neighborhood on Columbus's Near East Side, launching the $25.9 million Poindexter Village African American Museum — the adaptive reuse of two surviving buildings from the original 1940 federal public housing complex. The museum will include exhibition halls, an oral history studio, a research lab, an art gallery, and community gathering spaces. It is Ohio History Connection's 59th historic site. Target opening: early 2028. Architect: Moody Nolan (Columbus-based, nation's largest Black-owned architecture firm). Construction manager: Smoot Construction (Columbus-based). Total raised as of groundbreaking: $14.5 million of the $25.9M goal; CMHA announced $500K additional at the ceremony, bringing its total commitment to $1.5M.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: Cultural anchors at institutional scale — $25.9M, backed by the state's official history organization and CMHA — are the kind of investment that precedes and accelerates adjacent residential and retail activity. King-Lincoln Bronzeville has been absorbing significant Near East Side development attention; the museum's 2028 opening is the next milestone for operators and investors tracking that corridor.
- **Source(s)**: [WOSU — Ohio History Connection breaks ground on Poindexter Village African American Museum (May 6, 2026)](https://www.wosu.org/news/2026-05-06/ohio-history-connection-breaks-ground-on-poindexter-village-african-american-museum) (canonical), [Knox Pages / AP (May 6, 2026)](https://www.knoxpages.com/2026/05/06/ohio-history-connection-breaks-ground-on-25-9m-poindexter-village-african-american-museum-in-columbus/), [Axios Columbus (Feb. 25, 2026)](https://www.axios.com/local/columbus/2026/02/25/poindexter-village-museum-black-history), [Hoodline (Feb. 2026)](https://hoodline.com/2026/02/near-east-side-landmark-poised-for-second-life-as-poindexter-village-museum/), [Columbus Underground](https://columbusunderground.com/groundbreaking-scheduled-for-poindexter-village-museum-bw1/), [Poindexter Village Museum — Project Update](https://poindextervillagemuseum.org/museum-update)

### 2. COTA Launches TOD Study for Downtown Greyhound Terminal — Bids Due Today

- **What**: COTA is moving forward with a study to determine the best use of the former Greyhound bus terminal at 111 East Town Street — a 2.5-acre downtown property the agency has owned since approximately 2021, empty since buses stopped rolling in 2022. Bids for a transit-oriented development advisor and preliminary architecture/engineering services closed today (May 7, 2026). A $900,000 Build America Bureau federal grant is funding the work. COTA's board is expected to approve a contract in July 2026; consultant findings are targeted for mid-to-late 2027. COTA has cautioned that demolition, permitting, and financing would push actual buildout to the "later 2020s."
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: A 2.5-acre infill parcel at the core of downtown Columbus entering active TOD planning — however early-stage — is one of the most significant downtown land events in the current cycle. This is the companion story to the May 5 LinkUS BRT delay post: COTA's two big near-term Columbus moves are a troubled BRT delay and the launch of a downtown TOD study. The July board vote on the consultant contract is the next actionable date.
- **Source(s)**: [WOSU — COTA examines best use for former Greyhound bus terminal downtown (May 1, 2026)](https://www.wosu.org/2026-05-01/cota-examines-best-use-for-former-greyhound-bus-terminal-downtown) (canonical), [Axios Columbus — COTA continues planning for old Greyhound station's redevelopment (April 21, 2026)](https://www.axios.com/local/columbus/2026/04/21/greyhound-station-redevelopment-study-downtown-2026), [Columbus Underground — COTA Takes Step Toward Redeveloping Downtown Greyhound Station](https://columbusunderground.com/cota-takes-step-toward-redeveloping-downtown-greyhound-station-bw1/), [Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/04/columbus-ghost-greyhound-station-inches-toward-downtown-comeback/)

### 3. Ohio Stater Hotel Redevelopment Proposed for 1060 N. High St., University District

- **What**: A new proposal has been submitted to the City of Columbus to build a seven-story, 270-unit mixed-use development at 1060 N. High St. in the University District, requiring demolition of the Ohio Stater — a former hotel turned apartment complex at the corner of High and East Woodruff Ave. The Ohio Stater is scheduled to close in October 2026 for redevelopment. The proposed building would include 865 student-oriented beds, 9,000 SF of ground-floor retail, three interior courtyards (one with a pool), and a 260-space parking garage.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: The University District along N. High Street is one of the most active student housing and mixed-use corridors in Columbus. A 270-unit/865-bed proposal requiring demolition of a long-standing High Street landmark tests the neighborhood's current appetite for infill density — and the city's willingness to approve it under the new Zone In framework.

### 5. Zone In Phase 2 Implementation: New Zoning Maps Active Across 43% of Columbus

- **What**: Columbus City Council passed Zone In Phase 2 in June 2025, affecting approximately 66,000 properties — 43% of city parcels — with new mixed-use, industrial, and institutional zoning districts. Implementation of the new zoning maps is ongoing in early-mid 2026. Phase 3 (single-family residential districts) is not expected to begin until 2027. A February 2026 analysis by Realist Capital identified underpriced density plays created by the Phase 2 map that haven't yet been exploited by developers.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: The Phase 2 maps have been active for months, but many Columbus operators haven't fully mapped their portfolios against the new by-right envelope. The "hidden density" angle — parcels that now have more entitlement capacity than the market has priced in — is an undercovered story with direct investment relevance.
- **Source(s)**: [Columbus Underground — Next Phase of Zoning Reform Will Cover 43% of City](https://columbusunderground.com/next-phase-of-zoning-reform-will-cover-43-of-city-bw1/), [10TV — Columbus City Council greenlights next phase](https://www.10tv.com/article/news/local/columbus-city-council-approves-phase-two-zone-in-initiative/530-777ab85d-46d5-4974-89fa-ad20ee8f27fd), [Realist Capital — Capitalizing on the New Zoning Map: Where to Find the Hidden Density in Phase 2 (Feb. 2026)](https://www.realistcapital.com/2026/02/13/capitalizing-new-zoning-map-hidden-density-phase-2/)

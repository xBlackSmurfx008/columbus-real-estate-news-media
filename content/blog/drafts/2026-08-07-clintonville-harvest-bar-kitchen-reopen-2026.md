# Clintonville's Harvest Bar + Kitchen Reopens After Two Fires in Two Years

Harvest Bar + Kitchen is open again in Clintonville.

The pizza kitchen at 2885 N. High St. reopened after suffering its second fire in two years. The most recent fire involved a dumpster fire behind the building, with damage contained to the refrigeration unit and dumpsters, according to NBC4.

It is a comeback story for one of Clintonville's stalwart neighborhood spots.

## Two Fires, One Restaurant

The first fire hit Harvest Bar + Kitchen in 2022. The restaurant recovered and reopened.

Then, in July 2024, crews responded to another fire behind the building. That closure was shorter. The restaurant resumed service after repairs.

Two fire incidents in two years would shutter many small businesses. Harvest stayed in the fight.

## What Harvest Bar + Kitchen Is

Harvest Bar + Kitchen sits on N. High St. in Clintonville, the neighborhood's main commercial spine north of Henderson Road. It is a pizza-forward spot with a bar program — the kind of neighborhood anchor that Clintonville residents count on for casual weeknights.

The restaurant is co-founded by Chris Crader and Bethany Lovell, according to Columbus Underground.

## Why Clintonville Retail Resilience Matters

Clintonville's commercial corridors have stayed competitive as other city neighborhoods have seen retail turnover accelerate. N. High St. through Clintonville supports a dense mix of independent restaurants, coffee shops, and small retail — the kind of ecosystem that keeps residential demand strong.

When an anchor business like Harvest stays put through adversity, it sends a signal: this corridor holds. That matters to property owners and investors who hold commercial space on the strip.

For residents and neighbors: Harvest is back. Walk in Thursday at 11 a.m.

#### 5. ADU how-to questions (sewer cost barrier) — STEADY | Sentiment: CURIOUS / FRUSTRATED
Since the December 2025 ADU ordinance legalized accessory dwelling units in most Columbus residential districts, homeowners have been asking practical questions — most prominently about a cost barrier the city has not yet resolved: Columbus code requires ADUs to connect to city sewer independently from the main home, which can cost tens of thousands of dollars in excavation. A June 2026 Greater Ohio Policy Center study highlighted this specifically.

**Frame: CONTENT IDEA (practical explainer: what does an ADU actually cost in Columbus? The sewer question, step by step)**

Representative quotes:
- "Columbus city code says an ADU must connect to city sewer lines independently from the main home on a property, which could potentially cost tens of thousands in construction costs by requiring tearing up driveways or other parts of the property." — WOSU, June 2026 (source: wosu.org/politics-government/2026-06-18/study-shows-how-columbus-other-ohio-cities-could-ease-costs-of-building-accessory-dwelling-units)
- Multiple homeowner-oriented blogs (comptonllc.com, jsbrowncompany.com) have published Columbus ADU guides in 2026, indicating sustained question volume from homeowners researching the process. (Volume signal from distinct author/publisher count, not single-source repeats.)

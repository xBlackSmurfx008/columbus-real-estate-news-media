**Coverage signal:** Cover today ✓ (neighborhood slot 2/2 for week Aug 17–23)
**Confidence:** 4/5
**What:** Continental Development Ventures opened The Fitzgerald at 2200 E. Main St. in Bexley on August 14. The mixed-use building delivers 226 apartments (studio through 3BR) on a roughly three-acre former Trinity Lutheran Seminary site, with approximately 11,000 sq ft of ground-floor restaurant/retail space, 12,500 sq ft of office, and a 300-space structured parking garage. Coastal Ridge Real Estate (Columbus-founded 2013, $6.1B+ portfolio, 43K+ units) manages the property. No commercial tenants announced yet. Rents start around $1,432.
**Why it matters:** Largest new residential addition to Bexley in recent years. 226 new households walking E. Bexley has a new building at 2200 E. Main St. — and it's worth understanding what it brings beyond the apartment count.

The Fitzgerald opened August 14 with 226 studio-to-three-bedroom apartments on the site of the former Trinity Lutheran Seminary, plus:
- ~11,000 sq ft of ground-floor restaurant and retail space
- 12,500 sq ft of office space
- 300 structured parking spaces

The developer is Columbus-based Continental Development Ventures. Coastal Ridge Real Estate, a Columbus-founded firm that manages more than 43,000 units nationally, is running the property.

What makes this interesting from a neighborhood standpoint isn't the apartment count alone — it's the commercial inventory. E. Main St. through downtown Bexley already has an established strip of locally owned restaurants and retailers. Adding 23,500 square feet of new commercial space is the largest retail/office addition the corridor has seen in some time. Who fills those storefronts will shape what Bexley's Main Street looks like for the next decade.

No commercial tenants have been announced yet. That's the number to watch.

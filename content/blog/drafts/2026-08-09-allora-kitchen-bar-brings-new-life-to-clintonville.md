Clintonville's High Street has a new restaurant. [Allora Kitchen & Bar](https://allorakitchenbar.com/) opened at 4215 N. High St. this spring, stepping into the space that longtime locals knew as Gallo's on High.

The change is more rebrand than replacement. The new owner, Nizar Sta, took over the space and kept the restaurant running through the transition. Many Gallo's-era dishes stayed on the menu. New ones joined them.

## What's on the Menu

Sta has added Mediterranean and North African dishes alongside the Italian and American classics the space was known for. His Tunisian heritage shapes the additions — the kitchen is scratch-based, and Sta sources local products when he can, according to [Columbus Underground](https://columbusunderground.com/allora-kitchen-bar-takes-over-clintonville-bistro-ak1/).

Craft cocktails run $10 to $20.

Hours are Monday through Saturday from 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. and Sunday from 4 to 9 p.m.

## The Clintonville Signal

Allora is one of several new or rebranded operators to open along Clintonville's High Street stretch in 2026. See also [our recent coverage of Harvest Bar + Kitchen](/blog/clintonville-harvest-bar-kitchen-reopen-2026), which opened nearby this summer.

That concentration of new tenants is a commercial real estate signal. New operators choosing Clintonville over pricier corridors like Short North or German Village are doing so for a reason — lower rents, a loyal neighborhood customer base, and room to build an audience without a Short North landlord.

Rising tenant demand in a corridor tends to tighten vacancy and push rents upward over the following 12 to 18 months. Landlords and investors tracking the Clintonville High Street strip should note the trend.

The Gallo's on High space never went dark through the transition. Sta kept it open as the rebrand took shape, a sign of operational confidence. [Clintonville Spotlight](https://www.clintonvillespotlight.com/articles/allora-brings-new-brand-to-familiar-space/) and [614NOW](https://614now.com/2026/food-drink/new-italian-restaurant-opens-in-gallos-on-high-space) both covered the opening. [NBC4](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/new-restaurant-takes-over-former-gallos-on-high-in-clintonville/) confirmed location and ownership details.

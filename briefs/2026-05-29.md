### 1. Opportunity Pointe — Demolition Underway on Cleveland Avenue for 166-Unit Affordable Complex

- **What**: Columbus State Community Partners and Woda Cooper Companies are demolishing two buildings at 145 and 159 Cleveland Ave. this week — with work expected to complete by the end of the week — clearing the site for Opportunity Pointe, a $54 million, 166-unit affordable apartment complex at the corner of Cleveland Avenue and East Long Street, directly across from Columbus State's main downtown campus. Construction starts immediately after demolition. Project completion is targeted for end of 2027.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: This is one of the largest affordable housing projects to break ground near downtown Columbus in 2026: LIHTC rents from $365/month for a 1BR, 20 units reserved for CSCC students, and a two-institution partnership model (college + specialist LIHTC developer) that Columbus affordable housing watchers will want to track for replication. It adds durable income-restricted supply in a Near East Side corridor facing steady market-rate pressure.
- **Source(s)**: [Buildings Demolished to Make Way for New Housing Downtown](https://columbusunderground.com/buildings-demolished-to-make-way-for-new-housing-downtown-bw1/) (Columbus Underground, canonical), [Housing Planned for Key Corner Across From Columbus State Campus](https://columbusunderground.com/housing-planned-for-key-corner-across-from-columbus-state-campus-bw1/) (Columbus Underground), [Columbus State, Woda Cooper partnership proposes 160-unit workforce housing development](https://www.cscc.edu/about/news/2024/opportunity-pointe.shtml) (CSCC), [Opportunity Pointe moving forward with state tax credit approval](https://www.cscc.edu/about/news/2025/OpportunityPointeMay2025.shtml) (CSCC), [Affordable housing development to be built near Columbus State](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/affordable-housing-development-to-be-built-near-columbus-state/) (NBC4)

### 2. Edwards Companies Shelves $600M Capitol Square Renaissance — Indefinitely

- **What**: Edwards Companies CEO Jeff Edwards announced in April 2026 that the Capitol Square Renaissance project — a planned 10-acre mixed-use overhaul south of East Broad Street featuring 1,000+ residential units, nine restaurants, and nearly 200,000 sq ft of office space — is being put on hold indefinitely due to current market conditions. "While we remain confident in the long-term trajectory of downtown Columbus, today's market conditions indicate that now is not the right time for this project," Edwards said. Previously completed components include The Pembroke apartments and the Preston Centre redevelopment. No timeline for reconsideration was given.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: The $600M Capitol Square Renaissance was the single largest planned infill development in downtown Columbus; its shelving leaves a significant gap in the downtown pipeline and signals that even well-capitalized local developers are hitting financing and demand headwinds — a sobering data point for investors underwriting new downtown multifamily and mixed-use in 2026.

**5. Landmark on Scioto: 321-Unit Affordable Housing Breaks Ground (April 8); Leasing Summer 2026**  
Volume: **STEADY** | Sentiment: **Positive (housing advocates) / Cautious (policy observers: 60% AMI vs. ELI gap)**  
Frame: **Content idea** — Lincoln Avenue Communities (LAC) broke ground April 8, 2026 on Landmark on Scioto, a 321-unit build-to-rent affordable community on 44.5 acres in Columbus. Units are 2-, 3-, and 4-bedroom homes available to households earning up to 60% of Area Median Income (AMI). Amenities include fitness center, pool, clubhouse, outdoor spaces; LAC is also dedicating ~7 acres to the City of Columbus for a future public park. First leasing expected summer 2026; full build-out targeted for 2028. This is LAC's first ground-up construction project in Ohio. The project is positive supply news, but the policy tension is significant: at 60% AMI, Landmark on Scioto serves households earning roughly $45,000–$55,000 — not the extremely low-income (ELI) renters COHHIO identified as most cost-burdened (73% spending 50%+ of income on rent). No outlet has named that gap explicitly.

**1. ⚠️ April MLS Data Breaks Today: Closed Sales Down 13.6% While Prices Jump 8.3% — The Market is Splitting**  
Volume: **LOUD** (fresh official data, published today) | Sentiment: **Concerned (buyers) / Confused (market watchers)**  
Frame: **Threat + Content idea** — Columbus REALTORS published April 2026 MLS data today (May 14), covered immediately by WOSU. The headline number: **closed sales fell 13.6%** to 2,202 — the steepest single-month year-over-year drop in recent tracking. Year-to-date closings (7,617) are now down 4.2% through the first four months of 2026. At the same time, the **median sales price rose 8.3% to $346,500**, and the average hit $396,480 (up 5.9%). Inventory expanded to 5,027 homes (+13.4%), new listings rose 7.8%, months of supply crept to 2.0 (from 1.8), and days on market extended to 39 (from 32). This is the classic price-volume split: fewer transactions closing, but those that do close are clearing at significantly higher prices. For buyers, the story is mixed — more choice, but no relief on price. For the market narrative, this data undercuts the "Columbus is normalizing" framing from earlier in 2026 and raises a new question: if fewer buyers are transacting at ever-higher prices, where does the pressure come from when inventory keeps rising?

## What the data shows (all from CC0 City of Columbus open data, pulled Sept 27)

- **17 demolition permits** issued in August 2026 (9 one-to-three-family, 7 commercial,
  1 multifamily), across **13 distinct addresses**. Combined declared value **$360,000**.
- Monthly 2026: Jan 18, Feb 3, Mar 12, Apr 10, May 18, Jun 7, Jul 11, **Aug 17**. August
  is the year's second-highest month (Jan and May had 18); it is **not** a record.
- July 2026 = 11; August 2025 = 10.
- **ZIP 43207 (South Side)**: 6 of 13 sites and 10 of 17 permits — the largest share.
- Busiest single site: **2260 Lockbourne Rd** (South Side commercial), five permits,
  $80,000 combined. Only multifamily teardown: **1921-1931 Bairsford Dr** ($60,000).
- **Historic-district cross-check (original contribution):** a point-in-polygon test of
  all 13 sites against the City's 18 designated Historic Districts found **none** inside a
  district — no permitted teardowns of architecturally protected buildings in August.

Stated limitations in the article: permit counts are not project counts (one site can
carry several permits); declared value is the applicant's teardown estimate, not a sale
or assessed value; a permit is permission to demolish, not proof the building is gone;
the city updates the feed daily and late entries can lift a month's total.

# Columbus Real Estate News Media Taxonomy and Editorial Standards

## Brand Promise

Columbus-focused real estate and lifestyle intelligence for residents, buyers, sellers, investors, and local professionals.

## Information Architecture

Primary structure: `Area -> Topic -> Content Type`

## Areas

- Columbus (Citywide)
- Upper Arlington
- Dublin
- Grove City
- Westerville
- Hilliard
- New Albany
- Worthington
- Gahanna
- Bexley

## Topics

- Market Trends
- Neighborhood Guides
- Schools and Education
- Development and New Construction
- Local Politics and Policy (real-estate related)
- Restaurants and Entertainment
- Local Events
- Buying and Selling
- Investing and Rentals
- Home Services and Improvement

## Content Types

- Blog post
- News article
- Video
- Interview
- Case study
- Data brief

## Tagging Standard

Every published piece must include:

- One primary area
- One primary topic
- Up to three secondary topics
- One content type
- One audience tag (`resident`, `buyer`, `seller`, `investor`, `professional`)

## Editorial SOP

## 1) Intake and Planning

- Add topic idea to editorial queue.
- Assign primary area and topic before drafting.
- Mark intended monetization slot (`none`, `sponsor-safe`, `sponsored`).

## 2) Research

- Gather at least two reliable sources for factual claims.
- For schools, policy, and market stats, use official or primary sources whenever possible.
- Store source links in draft metadata.

## 3) Drafting

- Use a clear local angle in first two paragraphs.
- Include one actionable takeaway section.
- Include one internal link to area hub and one to topic hub.

## 4) Fact-Check and Compliance

- Verify all figures and dates.
- Label opinion content clearly.
- Add disclosure label for sponsored content.

## 5) SEO and Distribution Prep

- Optimize page title and meta description with area + topic keywords.
- Add newsletter CTA and follow-by-area CTA.
- Add UTM-tagged distribution links for social and email.

## 6) Publish and Measure

- Publish to site and assign to area/topic hubs.
- Track first 7-day performance (views, engagement, subscribes).
- Document learnings in weekly content retro.

## Writing Style Guide

- Tone: clear, local, practical, neutral.
- Reading level: plain language, short paragraphs.
- Avoid jargon unless defined.
- Always explain why local readers should care.

**3. "Our City Our Say" (Issue 8) ballot initiative — governance as a real estate driver**
**Volume: STEADY | Sentiment: CURIOUS / SUPPORTIVE (neighborhood advocates) / CAUTIOUS (city leadership)**
**Frame: Content opportunity (underreported angle for RE audience)**

Issue 8 will be on the November 2026 Columbus general election ballot. The initiative would amend the city charter so that only voters within each of Columbus's nine council districts can vote for their district representative — replacing the current at-large system where every Columbus voter votes for every council seat. Multiple area commissions have formally endorsed it (Far East, Livingston, University area commissions — all September 2026). NALC Buckeye Branch 78, Columbus Stand Up, Columbus Education Association, and Indivisible Central Ohio are among coalition members.

This is relevant to the CRE audience because district-only elections would significantly shift political power toward residential neighborhoods and away from citywide developer-aligned coalitions — potentially reshaping zoning vote outcomes and development approval dynamics for years.

**CRE News note:** The Oct. 24 Zone In Phase 2 comment close and the Nov. 2 Our Voice Our Choice vote are already in the coverage calendar; Issue 8 on Nov. 3 election day is not yet tracked separately. This is a gap.

**No direct verbatim social quotes sourced** — tracking via news reporting only. Volume signal: multiple distinct organizations endorsing publicly within the last 14 days.

**4. Housing affordability + eviction pressure**
**Volume: LOUD | Sentiment: NEGATIVE / FRUSTRATED**
**Frame: Content opportunity (ongoing beat)**

Columbus is on pace for approximately 25,000 eviction notices this year as pandemic rental assistance expires. The City announced a nearly $3 million prevention program (Mayor Ginther). Ohio Housing Finance Agency's FY26 assessment: only 58 affordable and available units exist for every 100 renters earning ≤50% AMI in Central Ohio. Adjusted for inflation, Ohio two-bedroom median rent rose 22% from December 2019 to December 2024.

City of Columbus is shifting from reactive to preventive housing support, per CWColumbus and ABC6 reporting. The ABC6 report (https://abc6onyourside.com/news/local/columbus-ohio-3-million-dollars-prevent-evictions-stabilize-families) noted the announcement of ~$3M in prevention investments and $1.5M for eviction legal help.

**No direct verbatim social quotes surfaced from distinct individual community members** (news coverage only). Volume signal: multiple distinct news outlets reporting, city action signaling severity.

**Content gap identified:** "Columbus eviction by the numbers: who is most at risk and what city/county resources are available right now?" — actionable explainer that could serve renters and landlords alike; higher trust play than outlets just summarizing the news.

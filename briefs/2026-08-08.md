### 1. NRP Group OSU East Groundbreaking — 336 Units, Northwest Columbus
- **What**: The NRP Group broke ground August 5, 2026 on OSU East, a 336-unit market-rate apartment community at West Dublin Granville Road in Northwest Columbus. Project: 11 three-story buildings, 1/2/3BR mix, 27.5-acre site within Worthington School District. Adjacent 138 for-sale townhomes by M/I Homes (groundbreaking spring 2027). 11-foot bike path with City of Columbus. Near Bridge Park, OSU Airport, Linworth Road Community Park.
- **Why it matters**: NRP Group's first market-rate Columbus project. National developer entry is a market signal for northwest corridor fundamentals. Combined 475-home development (rental + for-sale) on one site is a significant supply event.
- **Quote**: Mike Zelenkofske, EVP of Development, NRP Group: "This project represents an exciting milestone for our team and is the first of many opportunities we hope to pursue in Columbus."

## Social posts (Story 1 — NRP Group OSU East)

### LinkedIn
A national developer just broke ground in Columbus.

The NRP Group — one of the country's largest multifamily builders, with more than 60,000 homes built nationwide — started construction on August 5 on OSU East at West Dublin Granville Road in Northwest Columbus.

This is significant. OSU East is NRP Group's first market-rate project in Columbus. They have built affordable housing here before. Market-rate is different: no subsidies, no tax credits. The project only pencils out if Columbus rents and land values support the capital stack.

Columbus passed that test.

Here is what OSU East includes:
→ 336 apartments across 11 three-story buildings
→ One-, two-, and three-bedroom unit mix
→ 27.5-acre site within Worthington School District
→ 11-foot bike path built with City of Columbus
→ Near Bridge Park (Dublin), Linworth Road Community Park, OSU Airport
→ 138 for-sale townhomes by M/I Homes arriving spring 2027

Combined, the site will eventually add nearly 475 homes to Northwest Columbus.

Mike Zelenkofske, NRP Group's Executive Vice President of Development, said: "This project represents an exciting milestone for our team and is the first of many opportunities we hope to pursue in Columbus."

"First of many." When a developer of NRP Group's scale says that publicly, pay attention.

For Columbus operators near this corridor: 336 new market-rate units increase supply and tenant choice. That is competition. It also confirms that institutional capital sees northwest Columbus as a viable bet — which supports land values even as new supply enters the market.

Delivery expected late 2027 to early 2028 based on typical construction timelines.

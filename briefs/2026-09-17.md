Columbus's Italian Village just got its biggest multifamily approval in years.

The Italian Village Commission has approved a 428-unit apartment complex at 274 E. First Ave. — a seven-acre site that, until recently, housed the State Library of Ohio and the Ohioana Library. Developer Merus is bringing five architecturally distinct buildings to a site that was never in the residential development pipeline.

Three buildings will stand three stories; two will reach four. Groundbreaking is tentatively set for early 2027, with the first phase delivering in roughly two years.

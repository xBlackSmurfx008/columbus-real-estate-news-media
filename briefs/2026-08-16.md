**Chicago just paid $15.5 million for a Polaris strip center. Here's why that matters.**

Core Acquisitions, a Chicago-based real estate investment firm, acquired the Market at Polaris — 111,328 square feet at 1170 Polaris Pkwy. — for $15.5 million this week, according to Delaware County Auditor records first reported in the Colliers Columbus market digest.

That's $139 per square foot for a suburban Columbus retail property. To put that in context: earlier this month, a fully leased industrial campus near John Glenn Airport traded at $143 per square foot. The gap between well-anchored suburban retail and modern industrial in Columbus has nearly closed — something that would have seemed unusual just five years ago.

Alongside PetSmart and Big Sandy Furniture, the third anchor tenant is Slick City — an indoor slides and experiential entertainment concept that signed a 10-year initial lease at this property. Colliers noted the adjacent Cinemark ranks in the top 20 percent of locations nationally.

This is the Polaris corridor attracting capital across asset classes in 2026. Earlier this year, a Columbus investor bought two luxury apartment complexes nearby for $60 million. O'Connor Capital Partners picked up full ownership of Polaris Fashion Place. Now a Chicago firm is buying the adjacent strip.

For local operators and brokers: out-of-state buyers at measured price points signal market depth and genuine conviction in Columbus real estate — not just speculative flow. The story of Columbus commercial real estate in 2026 is partly about institutional capital looking for yield it can no longer find on the coasts, and finding it here.

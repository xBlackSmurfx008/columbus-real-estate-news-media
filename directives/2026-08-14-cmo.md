## What worked / what stalled

**Worked: the newsroom kept producing, and quality discipline went up.** Stories were staged every day from Aug 5 through Aug 13 with claim traceability, SEO metadata checks, and duplicate guards. Production and verification are not the constraint.

**Stalled: the site went dark for readers, and the approval loop was the cause.** 1 article published in the window against 13 staged. The pre-publish gate required a daily human review that never happened. The owner resolved this today by removing the gate. The lesson stands for everything below: any step that waits on a human inbox will stall. Automate the default, notify on Telegram, fix by exception.

**Stalled: we still have no ears.** With no pageview data we cannot tell a traffic problem from a conversion problem, so we cannot say which of the four funnels (FSBO, investor-sale, capital, renter) is weakest. All four zeros are indistinguishable. Per the owner's rule, empty tables mean awareness and instrumentation come before conversion work. Principle 17 says be quiet and listen. We have nothing to listen with. This is the fourth consecutive report directing it.

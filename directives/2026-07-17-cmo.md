## What worked / what stalled

**Worked — production capacity is real.** We have 37 live articles, and the pipeline proved it can run hot: the 2026-07-07 → 2026-07-10 stretch shipped two-plus articles a day across Development, Lifestyle, Market Analysis, and Economic Impact. The newsroom engine is not the constraint. When it runs, it produces.

**Stalled — the engine went dark.** Articles published in the window: **0**. The newest live article is 2026-07-10, so the daily cadence has been silent for seven straight days. Content is the only top-of-funnel awareness machine this property owns. When it stops, every funnel below it starves. This is the single most important number in the snapshot.

**Stalled — every funnel reads zero.** Leads across all four personas (FSBO, investor-sale, capital, renter): **0**. Members: **0**. Affiliate clicks: **0**. Subscribers and contacts are frozen at 1 each since March. The four funnel pages are built and live (`/sell/your-home`, `/sell/investment-property`, `/invest/deploy-capital`, `/rent/find-a-home`), so the problem is not missing infrastructure.

**Cannot yet name the weakest funnel — and that is the finding.** The task asks which of the four funnels underperforms and why (traffic, copy, placement, or offer). Honest answer: all four read 0, and we have **no traffic data to tell them apart.** We cannot see whether zero leads means zero visitors or a broken conversion path, because no pageview ever leaves the browser. Per the owner's rule, when the tables are empty we lead with traffic and awareness before conversion. That is exactly what the priorities below do: restart the awareness engine (P1), get eyes on traffic so we can diagnose (P2), then tighten conversion (P3).

## Directives (prioritized)

### P1 — Restore and harden the daily publishing cadence
- Why: KPI gap. Articles published in window = **0**; newest live article = 2026-07-10, seven days dark. Content is our only awareness engine, so a dark newsroom starves all four funnels upstream of anything conversion can fix. Sales principle 20 (strong marketing beats a strong product with no message) and 12 (be valuable first, then rare) — you cannot be valuable to a reader who never gets a new story.
- Definition of done: Diagnose why the cloud-scheduled daily run stopped after 2026-07-10 and restore it. The run publishes at least one real-estate article and one lifestyle article per day for seven consecutive days. Add a failure alert: if a scheduled run publishes 0 articles, the owner is pinged the same day (email or notification), so a silent seven-day gap can never recur unseen.

### P2 — Instrument real server-side traffic and funnel visibility
- Why: KPI gap plus sales principle 5 (ignore psychology and you market blind) and 17 (be quiet and listen). `trackEvent` writes only to `localStorage` and `console`, and the tracker is homepage-only, so the KPI report shows conversion outcomes with no traffic denominator. We literally cannot tell whether 0 leads is a traffic problem or a conversion problem. This must be non-annoying: privacy-friendly, no popups, no consent-wall theater.
- Definition of done: A pageview endpoint and table persist server-side views; the tracker is mounted on `blog/[slug]` and all four funnel pages; and `scripts/kpi-report.mjs` gains a "Traffic and funnel" section reporting pageviews, top articles, per-funnel-page views, and a view-to-lead rate per funnel. Next week's snapshot shows non-null pageviews for articles and each funnel page.

### P3 — Route every article into its matching funnel with an inline contextual CTA
- Why: KPI gap (leads = 0 across all four personas) and sales principles 9 (readers care what it does for them), 18 (use "you"/"your"), 19 (hook fast), and 25 (irresistible offer = proof + urgency + bonuses + risk reversal, but 23 outranks it — never invent proof or urgency). Article pages currently carry a generic CTA block, not an offer matched to the reader's intent. The upgrade must stay non-annoying: inline at the end of the article, never a popup or paywall.
- Definition of done: Each live article renders a category-matched inline funnel CTA (e.g. Development/Market/Economic → investor-sale and capital; Lifestyle/neighborhood → renter and FSBO) linking to the correct intake page, with click-through tracked via the P2 instrumentation. Target: first measurable leads and a non-zero view-to-lead rate within two weeks of P1 and P2 landing.

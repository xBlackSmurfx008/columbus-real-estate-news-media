# CREN Advertiser Outreach Package

As of: 2026-08-29
Purpose: convert the first 50-prospect advertiser list into sales-ready outreach while preserving CREN's editorial firewall and verified-audience posture.

## Sending Rules

- Use only verified contact names, role authority, and company contact paths before sending.
- Do not claim subscriber count, open rate, traffic scale, lead volume, lease volume, closing volume, or rankings unless independently verified.
- Do not promise editorial coverage, favorable coverage, rankings, or story placement.
- Label every paid placement clearly as advertising, sponsored content, or a paid profile upgrade.
- Keep regulated categories conservative: lending, insurance, legal, investment, brokerage, and capital-introduction offers need compliance review before claims or referral compensation.
- Store every touch in `docs/CREN_ADVERTISER_OUTREACH_TRACKER_2026-08-29.csv` or the approved CRM.

## Cadence

- Day 0: personalized first email.
- Day 2: follow-up with one specific CREN placement idea.
- Day 5: phone/voicemail or LinkedIn touch.
- Day 9: final short follow-up with a founding-sponsor angle.
- Day 21: recycle non-responders into a quarterly report/event sponsorship list.

## Apartment Operator Email

Subject options:

- Columbus renter demand and profile updates
- Founding apartment spotlight for Columbus Real Estate News
- Keep your Columbus apartment information current

Body:

```text

I am reaching out from Columbus Real Estate News. We are building CREN around Columbus housing intelligence: neighborhoods, apartment decisions, development tracking, market data, and practical renter/buyer resources.

For apartment operators, the first offer is simple: a verified apartment/community profile, last-updated availability notes, clear sponsor labeling, and campaign reporting on views, clicks, and renter inquiries.

Would it make sense to send a one-page founding apartment spotlight option for {{company}}?

Best,
{{sender_name}}
Columbus Real Estate News
```

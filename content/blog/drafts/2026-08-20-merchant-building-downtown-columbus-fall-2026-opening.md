# Merchant Building Is on Track to Open This Fall in Downtown Columbus

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Slug:** merchant-building-downtown-columbus-fall-2026-opening  
**Category:** development  
**Area:** downtown-columbus  
**Tags:** columbus-ohio, central-ohio-real-estate, downtown-columbus, development, multifamily, mixed-use, north-market

---

The 32-story Merchant Building in downtown Columbus is on schedule to complete in the fourth quarter of 2026. The tower will add 174 apartments, a 206-room hotel, 68,000 square feet of office space, and a 15,000-square-foot expansion of North Market to the city's downtown core.

## A parking lot held a cemetery

Construction started in 2023, and it started with a complication. When crews began clearing the gravel parking lot behind North Market, they found the lot had been built over a 19th-century cemetery — and not all the remains had been relocated when the cemetery closed, [according to NBC4](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/north-market-construction-update-when-transformational-project-could-be-finished/). The discovery delayed the project while the site was handled properly.

Three years later, the 32-story tower rises above the Short North. Nationwide Realty Investors (NRI) is the developer. Gilbane Building Co. is the general contractor. The schedule targets Q4 2026 — timed, not coincidentally, with North Market's 150th anniversary. The market first opened in 1876.

## What's in the building

The Merchant Building's program is unusually broad for a single Columbus address, according to reporting in [Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/merchant-building-downtown-columbus-north-market/719566/), [Construction Dive](https://www.constructiondive.com/news/merchant-building-downtown-columbus-north-market/718876/), and [Smart Cities Dive](https://www.smartcitiesdive.com/news/merchant-building-residential-downtown-columbus-north-market/719640/):

- **174 apartments** across multiple floor plans
- **206 hotel rooms** in what has been described as a luxury hotel with a private members' social club
- **68,000 square feet of Class A office space**
- **15,000 square feet** of new North Market space across two floors

The 174 apartments arrive in a thinning market. Columbus's multifamily pipeline is running 40–50% below 2025 delivery volumes this year, according to Multifamily Dive — meaning new units carry more weight than they would have twelve months ago.

## The North Market expansion

The new market space — 15,000 square feet across two floors — is designed to hold eight to nine new vendor stalls, a Market-owned bar (North Market already operates one at its Bridge Park location), an event space on the second floor overlooking a new outdoor plaza, and additional seating and restrooms, [according to NBC4](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/north-market-expansion-to-include-new-merchant-stalls-outdoor-plaza-event-space/).

North Market has named its first permanent merchant for the new space: Caroline's, a pop-up restaurant from Columbus chef Justin Wotring, will move into a permanent stall once construction completes. [The market announced](https://northmarket.org/carolines-pop-up-becomes-permanent-at-north-market-downtown-named-first-merchant-in-expansion-project/) that Columbus firm GRA+D Architects designed the expansion interior, and Ruscilli Construction is handling construction on that portion of the project.

Eight or nine new vendor stalls is a meaningful addition for a market that historically maintains a waitlist for permanent spots. Pairing the stalls with a bar and a second-floor event space — overlooking a redesigned outdoor plaza — suggests North Market is building for both daily traffic and private events as revenue streams.

## Streets are changing too

Columbus is investing $4.2 million to revamp the streets around North Market and the Merchant Building, [NBC4 reported in August 2025](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/columbus-invests-4-2-million-to-revamp-streets-around-north-market-32-story-tower/). The project covers pedestrian access improvements and plaza approach work, preparing the surrounding block for significantly higher foot traffic when the hotel and market expansion open.

That kind of public investment alongside a private development is worth noting. Large towers frequently open on blocks where streets haven't caught up — which slows the activation of ground-floor retail and market stalls. Here the city is moving in advance of the opening.

## What's still unknown

Several significant questions remain unanswered publicly: no hotel brand has been confirmed, no residential leasing details have been released, and no office tenant announcements have been made as of publication. The Q4 2026 schedule remains subject to change given the project's complexity.

What has been confirmed: Caroline's as the first market merchant, the expansion's eight-to-nine-stall count, the $4.2 million street investment, and the Q4 target. For anyone tracking downtown Columbus real estate — [residential, hospitality, and commercial](/areas/downtown-columbus) — the Merchant Building is the largest new mixed-use delivery expected in the downtown core this year.

A national developer broke ground on 336 apartments in Northwest Columbus on August 5, 2026.

The NRP Group started construction on OSU East, a market-rate apartment community at West Dublin Granville Road. It is the company's first market-rate project in Columbus, according to its August 5 press release. NRP Group has built more than 60,000 homes across the country.

"This project represents an exciting milestone for our team and is the first of many opportunities we hope to pursue in Columbus," said Mike Zelenkofske, Executive Vice President of Development at The NRP Group.

## What OSU East Includes

OSU East will have 11 three-story buildings. The unit mix covers one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments. Total: 336 homes.

The site spans 27.5 acres on West Dublin Granville Road. It sits within the Worthington School District — one of the most sought-after school catchments in central Ohio. That catchment commands premium rents and draws stable tenant demand.

M/I Homes of Central Ohio will build 138 for-sale townhomes on the same site. M/I Homes plans to break ground on its portion in spring 2027. The combined development will bring nearly 475 homes to the northwest corridor.

## Bike Path and Connections

As part of the project, the city of Columbus will help install an 11-foot-wide bike path along the development's street frontage. The path will connect residents to dining and retail along Dublin-Granville Road and to Bridge Park in Dublin — the mixed-use district about two miles west.

The site is also near Linworth Road Community Park, which has soccer fields, baseball diamonds, and walking trails. Ohio State University Airport is nearby as well.

## What This Entry Means for Columbus

National developers do not enter new markets on a hunch. NRP Group has built affordable housing in Ohio before. But OSU East is market-rate — no subsidies, no tax credits. The project only works if the rents here cover construction and land costs.

Columbus has been passing that test. Nationwide Realty Investors and Pizzuti Companies are already active Downtown. Dublin's Bridge North development — 296 homes, a hotel, and offices — received final approval in July 2026. OSU East adds institutional capital to the northwest corridor specifically.

That corridor connects Worthington's employment base to Dublin's retail and lifestyle infrastructure. It has strong schools, quick access to Interstate 270, and proximity to some of the highest household incomes in central Ohio.

## What It Means for Columbus Operators

For landlords near West Dublin Granville Road, 336 new market-rate units add supply to the northwest corridor. More supply creates more competition for tenants. Watch for that pressure when OSU East delivers — likely 2027 to 2028 based on typical construction timelines for a project this size.

For investors watching land values, NRP Group's entry is a data point. Institutional capital tends to confirm what local operators already know: this area has room to run.

The 138 M/I Homes for-sale townhomes give the site a mixed-tenure structure. A site with both renters and owners tends to stabilize a neighborhood faster than a single product type alone.

## What to Watch

**Delivery timeline.** NRP Group did not announce a completion date. Based on project scale, late 2027 to early 2028 is a reasonable estimate. Watch for leasing announcements.

**M/I Homes groundbreaking.** Spring 2027 is the stated target. If M/I starts on schedule, the combined development brings nearly 475 homes to one site.

**Bike path timeline.** The city partnership signals Columbus is investing in northwest corridor infrastructure. That matters for long-term land values and future development in the area.

NRP Group called Columbus "the first of many opportunities." For a developer of its scale, that is not a throwaway line. Columbus's fundamentals need to hold — and right now, they are.

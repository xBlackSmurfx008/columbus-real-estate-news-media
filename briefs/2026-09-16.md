### LinkedIn (for when live)

Columbus just broke ground on something most cities can't pull off: 23 condos at $199,000 each — and the price stays at $199,000 for every buyer, forever.

That's the Central Ohio Community Land Trust model. The city and COCLT held a groundbreaking at East Broad Street and South Gould Road on August 27. The project is the first visible deployment of the $500 million Affordable Housing Bond Columbus voters approved last November.

Here's how it works: COCLT retains ownership of the land in perpetuity. Buyers own their units, build equity, and can sell — but the resale price is calculated using a formula that keeps the next sale affordable and restricted to income-qualified buyers. The unit stays affordable through every transaction, not just the first. That's different from most affordable housing programs, where restrictions expire in 15-30 years.

Details on the project:
— 23 two- and three-bedroom condos, 950-1,300 sq ft
— ~$199,000 price point
— Income limit: 120% of area median income
— Completion target: fall 2027
— Financing: $3M from the city bond (construction) + $1.1M homebuyer assistance
— Partners: City of Columbus, COCLT, Finance Fund

The Columbus median sale price hit $350,000 in July — up 2.3% year-over-year. At $199K, these units are the entry-point the city's been promising. Whether the model can scale is the next question.

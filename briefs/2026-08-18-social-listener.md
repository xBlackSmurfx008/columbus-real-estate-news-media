### 2. Rent Wage Gap — "$27.50 an Hour to Afford Columbus Rent" — new specific figure confirmed — LOUD, frustrated/urgent

**Summary:** The Ohio Capital Journal (July 24, 2026) reported that Ohio renters need to earn at least $23.82/hr to afford a two-bedroom rental statewide — and that the Columbus-specific "Housing Wage" (the hourly rate needed to afford a modest 2BR without spending more than 30% of income) is **$27.50/hr**. The average Ohio renter earns $19.59/hr — a $4/hr+ gap statewide, and a larger gap in Columbus. Separately, the 2026 COHHIO Gap Report (March) confirmed: Columbus lacks 53,000 affordable units; only 27 affordable units exist for every 100 extremely low-income renters (households earning <$33,000/yr for a family of four); 73% of ELI renters spend more than half their income on housing. Multiple Ohio outlets (Ohio Capital Journal, Spectrum News 1, NBC4, Columbus Today/National Today, Vindicator editorial) covered the Gap Report in March 2026, and the wage-gap figure is still circulating in August via ABC6, NewsBreak, and other outlets.

**Volume signal:** Loud — at least six distinct outlets confirmed; the specific $27.50 housing wage figure is appearing in fresh August search results, suggesting continued social circulation.

**Sentiment:** Frustrated / urgent — advocates and renters are angry; the gap between the "affordable city" narrative and ELI renter reality is the dominant subtext.

**Representative quotes:**

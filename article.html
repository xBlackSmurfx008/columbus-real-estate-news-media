Dublin market pulse: inventory inches up while buyer demand remains active

Inventory has risen modestly in recent weeks while median sale prices remain stable. Buyers continue competing for homes in top school zones, but days on market have improved compared with last quarter.

What this means for residents

  • Sellers still benefit from strong demand in move-in-ready segments.
  • Buyers may have a slightly better negotiating position than in prior cycles.
  • Local service providers can align offers around spring listing activity.

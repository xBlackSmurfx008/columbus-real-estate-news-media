# Local Advertiser Outreach Cadence

## Weekly Quotas

- 40 new outreach messages
- 20 follow-ups
- 8 discovery calls requested
- 3 proposals sent
- 1 closed-won target

## Sequence (10 Business Days)

1. Day 1: Intro email or LinkedIn message
2. Day 3: Follow-up with area/topic audience fit
3. Day 6: Share one-page package summary and relevant placement examples
4. Day 8: Phone call or voice note follow-up
5. Day 10: Breakup message with next-quarter follow-up option

## Discovery Call Agenda (20 Minutes)

1. Business goals and target customers (5 min)
2. Geography and audience fit (5 min)
3. Placement recommendations and package options (7 min)
4. Next steps and timeline (3 min)

## Email Template

Subject: Reach [Area] homebuyers and residents in Columbus

Hi [Name],

I run a new Columbus real estate and lifestyle media platform with area-specific audience segments (Upper Arlington, Dublin, Grove City, and more). We are opening a limited number of founding advertiser spots that include website, newsletter, and sponsored content placements.

If helpful, I can share a quick 15-20 minute overview with audience fit and recommended placements for your business.

Would [Option A time] or [Option B time] work next week?

Thanks,  
[Your Name]

## Qualification Criteria

- Serves Columbus or nearby areas
- Clear fit with real estate/lifestyle readership
- Has budget and decision-maker involvement within 30 days

- Why: KPI gap — 0 funnel views and 0 CTA clicks across all four funnels for
  the fourteenth consecutive snapshot, against 77 pageviews that saw no
  lead-intake surface; meanwhile 15 affiliate clicks (up from 13) prove
  readers act on outbound links when they are shown one, and 5 of 15 are
  rental partners while the renter funnel sits at 0 views (KPI snapshot).
  Principles 13 (the offer isn't landing because it isn't placed), 19 (no
  hook without placement), and 9 (the reader never sees what CREN does for
  them). Operational record: the taxonomy-matched contextual CTA layer is
  complete on `main`; the remaining step is a durable deploy path, plus the
  commercial disclosure on four funnel pages (`disclosure-funnel` blocking
  finding, unchanged since Sep 20). This outranks P2 because lead intake is
  revenue priority #1, the demand signal grew again this week without it,
  and one deploy exposes the funnels, the membership prompt, and the
  disclosure fix simultaneously. No popups, no paywalls, no interruptive
  tactics — contextual placements only.
- Definition of done: (a) a durable deploy path exists — the Vercel project
  connected to this repo for deploy-on-push, or a deploy token / hook in
  the routine environment — proven by one production deploy of current
  `main`; (b) after the deploy, the taxonomy-matched contextual CTA renders
  on 100% of live article pages (spot-checked on at least one article per
  funnel taxonomy), and all four funnel pages render the commercial
  disclosure, clearing the `disclosure-funnel` finding in
  `verify:site -- --target production`; (c) at least one flagged test

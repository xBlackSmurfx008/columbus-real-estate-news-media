**3. Columbus Rental Registry — April Passage, Ongoing Polarization**  
Volume: **STEADY** | Sentiment: **Polarized** (landlord frustration / tenant advocate skepticism that it was weakened)  
Frame: **Threat (landlord audience) + Opportunity (renter/policy audience)** — Columbus City Council voted 8-1 on April 20, 2026 to establish the citywide rental registry; debate remains active among landlords, tenant groups, and property managers

The rental registry requires all landlords to register rental units with the city. The Columbus Apartment Association has stated intent to challenge the ordinance in court on state preemption grounds; no suit has been confirmed filed as of this scan. Tenant advocates, including BREAD, supported the registry in principle but publicly said the final version was weakened after mandatory unit inspections were removed due to legal concerns. The city retained preventative inspections of apartment building common areas every three years. Council sponsor Nick Bankston has maintained the legislation is designed to withstand challenge. RL Property Management's 2026 Columbus market report notes the registry as a compliance factor for landlords alongside the existing source-of-income protection ordinance (in effect since 2024).

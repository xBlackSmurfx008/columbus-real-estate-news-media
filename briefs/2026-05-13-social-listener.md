**4. BiggerPockets Q2 2026 Pulse: Investor Sentiment Falls Sharply; Columbus Still Rated Opportunity**  
Volume: **STEADY** | Sentiment: **Mixed (cautious but opportunistic)**  
Frame: **Opportunity + Threat** — The BiggerPockets Q2 2026 Investor Pulse Survey (234 respondents; fielded April 1–10) shows the forward-looking Pulse Index dropped from **150 in Q1 to 112 in Q2** — a meaningful pullback. Key drivers: fears about AI displacement in the labor market and the war in Iran. Investors have revised mortgage rate expectations upward to the 6.0%–6.49% range (from 5.5%–5.99% last quarter). Despite the sentiment drop, a majority still plan to grow their portfolios — citing better deal flow, improved negotiating leverage, and falling prices in some markets as counterweights. Columbus specifically is called out by multiple market analysts as one of the Midwest's most investor-stable metros, with sub-5% vacancy rates and above-average tenant retention. The tension: national investor caution + local tariff project freezes (Theme 1) may combine to create deal flow for well-capitalized buyers, while reducing new supply in a market that already needs more units.

> "Retail investors' hopes for improved market conditions are fading in the second quarter of 2026, but many investors still plan to actively grow their portfolios in an increasingly strong buyer's market." — BiggerPockets Pulse Q2 2026 ([biggerpockets.com/blog/pulse-q2-2026](https://www.biggerpockets.com/blog/pulse-q2-2026))

> "Much of that optimism has faded as the war in Iran has pushed up inflation and bond yields, taking mortgage rates up with them." — BiggerPockets Pulse Q2 2026 ([biggerpockets.com/blog/pulse-q2-2026](https://www.biggerpockets.com/blog/pulse-q2-2026))

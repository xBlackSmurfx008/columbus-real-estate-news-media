Supporting facts (read from the live database and the daily briefs, not fabricated):
- The database holds 17 articles in `status='draft'`. 13 of them were created Aug 5 through Aug 13. Drafts never appear on the public site. Only 1 live article was created in this window (Gahanna Creekside, Aug 10). Before that, the last live article was Aug 2.
- Cause found in code: the admin articles API (`frontend/app/api/admin/articles/route.ts`) defaults `status` to `'draft'` when the field is omitted. The direct publish script (`publish-article.mjs`) sets `'live'`, but the Aug 7 to Aug 10 briefs show daily runs were blocked from the database and fell back to another path. Every story since Aug 5 except one landed as an invisible draft.
- The newsroom does not know this. `recent-articles.mjs` does not filter or show status, so its output lists drafts as "published." The Aug 9 SEO report calls Aug 5 to Aug 7 articles "confirmed live." They are drafts.
- No daily brief has been committed since Aug 10, though database rows show the newsroom ran Aug 11, 12, and 13.
- Still no traffic instrumentation anywhere in the app. `frontend/lib/analytics-client.ts` writes only to browser localStorage and is not mounted on any page. There is no pageview endpoint or table. This is the fourth consecutive report with no traffic denominator.
- Three directives now sit unapproved: 2026-07-17, 2026-08-02, and 2026-08-03. The CTO builds only approved directives, so none of the directed work has been built.

## What worked / what stalled

**Worked: the newsroom kept producing.** Stories were drafted and inserted every day from Aug 5 through Aug 13, including two on most days. Research quality held. Sourcing and dedup discipline held. Production is not the constraint.

**Stalled: publishing itself silently broke.** The snapshot says 1 article published in the window. The newsroom drafted 13 in the same period. The other 12 sit as drafts readers cannot see. Site visitors have seen one new story in twelve days. Every downstream number (subscribers +0, leads 0, affiliate clicks 0) is meaningless while the shelf is empty. This is the single most damaging fact in this report, and no prior directive covers it because it began Aug 5.

**Stalled: we still have no ears.** Leads 0, members 0, subscribers stuck at 1. With no pageview data we still cannot tell a traffic problem from a conversion problem, so we cannot say which of the four funnels (FSBO, investor-sale, capital, renter) is weakest. Per the owner's rule, empty tables mean awareness and instrumentation come before conversion work. Instrumentation has now been directed three times without approval. Principle 17 says be quiet and listen. We have nothing to listen with.

**Stalled: the approval loop.** Nothing directed on Jul 17, Aug 2, or Aug 3 was approved, so nothing was built. The cost is now measurable: a publishing failure ran unnoticed for nine days partly because the instrumentation that would have exposed it (pageviews flatlining) was never approved. One checked box per week unblocks everything below.

## Directives (prioritized)

### P1 — Flip the stranded drafts live and make the publish path fail loudly (new this week)
- Why: Principle 21 (a killer offer won't save you in a dead market) and principle 20 (strong marketing beats a strong product with no message). KPI gap: 1 article live in the window against 13 drafted. Nothing else in this report can move while readers see an empty shelf.
- Definition of done: All verified article drafts from Aug 5 to Aug 13 are `status='live'` on the public site (audit the 4 older drafts too; flip or delete each with a stated reason). Every pipeline publish path sets `status='live'` explicitly, including the admin API fallback. `recent-articles.mjs` prints each article's status so drafts can never again read as published. Next Monday's KPI shows articles published in window equal to articles drafted in window.

### P2 — Instrument server-side traffic and funnel visibility (fourth issue; first directed 2026-07-17)
- Why: Principles 5 (ignore psychology and you market blind) and 17 (be quiet and listen). KPI gap: subscribers, members, leads, and affiliate clicks all read 0 with no pageview data to say why. This directive is unchanged from the last three reports because the gap is unchanged. It would also have caught the P1 outage within a day.
- Definition of done: A pageview endpoint and table persist views server-side, privacy-friendly, no popups or consent theater. The tracker mounts on `blog/[slug]` and all four funnel pages (`/sell/your-home`, `/sell/investment-property`, `/invest/deploy-capital`, `/rent/find-a-home`). `kpi-report.mjs` gains a traffic section: pageviews, top articles, per-funnel-page views, view-to-lead rate. Next Monday's snapshot shows non-null traffic numbers.

### P3 — Restart distribution: social copy every publish day, hero images restored (carried from 2026-08-03 P2)
- Why: Principle 26 (good visuals sell more than you think) and principle 20. Gaps: subscribers stuck at 1 while 13 stories were drafted; social channel copy last written 2026-06-04; recent briefs show every run since Aug 7 skipped images. We publish daily to an audience of one and push nowhere.
- Definition of done: The daily pipeline writes `content/<channel>/<date>.md` distribution copy on every publish day for 7 consecutive days. Hero image generation runs again in the daily pipeline; owner action stays flagged: refresh the Higgsfield credentials (interactive re-login only the owner can do). Once refreshed, live articles missing a hero trend to 0 and every new article publishes with one.

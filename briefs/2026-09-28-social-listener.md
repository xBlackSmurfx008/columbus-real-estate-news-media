## Lead 1 — BJ's Wholesale Club Files for Planned Grove City Store at Stringtown Road

**Summary:** BJ's Wholesale Club has filed a preliminary development application for a 104,470-square-foot membership warehouse and 18-pump gas station at 1209–1213 Stringtown Road in Grove City. The roughly 18-acre site sits between Buckeye Parkway and Jackson Pike (State Route 104). The proposal includes 422 parking spaces, a 400-square-foot kiosk, and approximately 1.2 acres reserved along Stringtown Road for a potential retail or restaurant outparcel with drive-thru capability. Three access points are proposed: two full-service and one right-in, right-out. If approved, this would be the company's third Central Ohio location (after New Albany in 2022 and Lewis Center in 2023) and its ninth Ohio store. BJ's, headquartered in Massachusetts, operates 267 clubs across 22 states; the company is also constructing a distribution center in Commercial Point (Franklin County township, south of Columbus) expected to open in early 2027. The project requires Grove City Council approval for a planned-unit commercial zoning change: the parcel currently straddles industrial zoning in Grove City and suburban residential zoning in Jackson Township. No public hearing date or construction schedule has been announced.

**Why this matters for CREN (commercial/suburban real estate angle):**
- A membership warehouse proposal of this scale (18 acres, 104,000+ sq ft, dual municipality footprint) represents a significant suburban commercial land-use decision — converting a mixed industrial/residential parcel to planned commercial at the SW Columbus edge.
- The zoning conversion request (industrial + suburban residential → commercial PUD) on land split across two jurisdictions (Grove City and Jackson Township) is a procedurally interesting real estate story: it requires coordinated approval, not just a single city vote.
- Grove City is an active growth corridor for Columbus metro commercial development; BJ's third Central Ohio opening would test whether the market supports three warehouse clubs (Costco, Sam's Club, BJ's) in a mid-size metro.
- The BJ's distribution center opening in Commercial Point (early 2027) ties the individual store proposal to a broader Central Ohio supply chain infrastructure story.

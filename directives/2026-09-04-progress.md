## Findings surfaced during the build

- **A fabricated provenance line was live in the product.** The retired
  `lib/market-snapshot.ts` rendered "Updated 6 hours ago | Source: Local MLS
  aggregates" over a hardcoded static array. There is no MLS aggregate feed.
  It is deleted; every metric now carries real source and date or is not shown.
- **The stale 5,223 / +8.2% / 6.43% figures** came from
  `columbusrealestatenews-v2.html` / `-v3.html` at the repo root. Deleted, plus
  301 redirects so the indexed URLs actually de-index rather than lingering.
- **`chatgpt.com` is sending real referral traffic** (3 views / 2 visitors this
  window) — never previously reported. "Direct/none" is 60% of all traffic and
  is worth attributing properly.
- **The `crm-` source prefix is now reserved for test traffic.** A future real
  CRM integration must not name its source `crm-*`. Documented in
  `frontend/docs/TEST_TRAFFIC_CONVENTION.md`.

## Outreach Sequence

1. Apartment/property managers: lead with apartment profile claim, stale availability prevention, and renter-intent reporting.
2. Developers/builders: lead with development tracker, buyer guide, and report/event sponsorship.
3. Movers/storage: lead with OSU move-in and renter checklist sponsorship.
4. Inspectors/title/lenders/legal: lead with buyer/seller due-diligence guides and strict claim-review standards.
5. Home-service vendors: lead with directory claim, service areas, credentials, and sponsored service guides.

## Sales Qualification Questions

- Which Columbus geographies, projects, communities, or customer intents matter this quarter?
- Is the goal awareness, profile actions, direct inquiries, tour/application clicks, report sponsorship, event sponsorship, or renewal pipeline?
- What objective claims must appear in the creative?
- Who can approve claim substantiation, fair-housing-sensitive copy, or regulated-service disclosures?
- Is lead routing requested, and under what compensation model?
- What report cadence would make renewal easier: weekly, monthly, end-of-flight, or quarterly?

## What worked / what stalled

### What worked

- **The dated-peg play worked as designed.** Observation: the Reserve at
  Maryland article went live Sep 12, four days ahead of its Sep 16 event peg
  (brief record; last week's P2). Its page drew views inside the window only
  via the article set at large; no per-article claim is made beyond the top
  pages table. Publishing ahead of a dated event is the property's only
  measured traffic amplifier (CLAUDE.md), and this week the newsroom executed
  it on schedule.
- **The editorial safety net caught a real duplicate.** Operational record
  (Sep 13 Session 2 brief, not a KPI): the deterministic gate plus a
  credentialed recent-articles check stopped a duplicate Green Oaks story
  before publish. Principle 23 (never sacrifice long-term trust): the
  property's credibility bar held under a degraded pipeline.
- **Search remains the property's channel.** Observation: www.google.com sent
  40 views / 25 visitors, 47.1% of the window's 85 pageviews, and 7 of the
  top 10 pages are article pages (KPI snapshot). The content-to-search engine
  works; what readers land on still has no conversion layer (see stalled).
  search.yahoo.com's 12 views come from 2 visitors — one or two readers, not
  a channel.
- **Zero forced weak stories.** Observation: Articles published in window = 6
  (KPI snapshot); brief record: Sep 13–14 published zero rather than force a
  duplicate or single-sourced item, per the CLAUDE.md standard. This is
  discipline working, though it has a traffic cost (see stalled).

### What stalled

- **The conversion layer has now missed eight consecutive snapshots.**
  Observation: 0 views, 0 CTA clicks, 0 form starts, 0 submissions on all
  four funnels — FSBO, investor-sale, capital, AND renter this week — across
  85 pageviews (KPI snapshot). Business implication: 100% of the week's
  readers saw no revenue mechanism at all; lead intake is revenue priority
  #1 and it received zero exposure (principles 9, 13, 19). Hypothesis
  (unchanged, diagnosed in the Sep 11 brief record): the conversion build is
  correct on `main`, production runs an older build, and no automated session
  has a deploy path. The KPI proves the exposure gap; the deploy diagnosis is
  operational record, not KPI.
- **Publishing volume fell and traffic fell with it.** Observation: Articles
  published in window dropped to 6 from 8 in the 2026-09-12 snapshot, with
  zero published Sep 13–14; daily pageviews decayed 21 → 21 → 16 → 9 → 5 → 5
  across Sep 8–13, and the window total fell to 85 from 131 (KPI snapshots;
  the two windows overlap five days, so this is a trailing-window shift, not
  a clean week-over-week comparison). Business implication: with no
  conversion layer, traffic is the only asset the property is building, and
  it is decaying. Hypothesis: the blind AM pipeline (14 consecutive runs

  (stranded drafts) and indirectly (duplicate risk forces conservative
  zero-publish calls); normal indexing lag on a young article set is equally
  plausible. The KPI data does not identify the cause.

  Observation: Leads total 1, new in window +0, status `renter / new`,
  Contacted 0, Qualified 0, Avg first response n/a (KPI snapshot). The lead

- **Membership, contact, affiliate, and activation lines are flat at zero.**

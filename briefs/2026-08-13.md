### Article 2 — Welcome Home Ohio $45.6M

**LinkedIn (1,200–2,200 chars):**
Ohio opened a $45.6 million grant round today that Columbus nonprofits and city/county officials can apply for — and the timing is worth noting.

The Welcome Home Ohio program splits the $45.6M into two equal pools: $22.8M for purchasing foreclosed homes before they go to investors, and $22.8M for rehabbing existing homes or building new ones. Both pools require that properties ultimately be sold to income-qualified buyers. This is a homeownership program, not a rental subsidy.

Who can apply: qualified nonprofit community development organizations and local government subdivisions — including the City of Columbus and Franklin County.

Why it matters right now: Franklin County's 30-year mortgage rate hit 6.69% last week — a yearlong high. Columbus's median sale price was $329,900 in the most recent Franklin County Auditor weekly report. At that combination of rate and price, programs that directly lower the purchase or rehab cost for lower-income buyers are among the few tools available. Columbus CDCs have used prior Welcome Home Ohio rounds to do exactly this work: acquire foreclosed homes in neighborhoods like Franklinton, the Near East Side, and Linden, and sell them to first-time buyers at prices the market alone wouldn't produce.

This round is running concurrently with Ohio's $120M Brownfield Remediation application window (open through September 11). If your organization is active in Columbus-area community development, both programs are worth reviewing this month.

Full details and program guidelines at development.ohio.gov. Link in bio.

## What worked / what stalled

**Worked: the publish-live policy ended the dark-site problem.** 12 articles published in this window against 1 in the prior window. Production, verification, and cadence are no longer the constraint.

**Worked: the hero backlog is nearly cleared.** Live articles with a broken hero went from ~17 (Aug 15 brief) to 1 (today's audit of all 74 live articles). Last week's P3 is close to its "zero and stays there" target.

**Stalled: nothing converted.** 12 stories shipped and every audience number stayed flat: subscribers 1 (+0), free members 0, leads 0 across all four personas, affiliate clicks 0, contact messages +0. Publishing alone is not moving anyone.

**Stalled: we still cannot tell which funnel is underperforming, or why.** With zero pageview data, FSBO, investor-sale, capital, and renter all read the same: 0. We cannot distinguish "no one visits the funnel pages" (a traffic problem) from "visitors bounce off the copy or offer" (a conversion problem). Principle 17 says be quiet and listen; we still have nothing to listen with. Per the owner's rule, empty tables mean awareness and instrumentation come before conversion work. This is the fifth report saying so, which itself is the strongest argument to build it now.

**Stalled: the pipeline fails silently.** The cloud routine had no database access 5 of 7 days and nobody was told. A human noticed days later. Today's article is still sitting on disk. The Telegram channel the owner asked for on Aug 14 is half built; it would have surfaced the first failure the same morning.

## Directives (prioritized)

### P1 — Instrument server-side traffic and funnel visibility (fifth issue; first directed 2026-07-17)
- Why: Principles 5 (ignore psychology and you market blind) and 17 (be quiet and listen). KPI gap: leads, members, and affiliate clicks all read 0 while 12 articles published; without a traffic denominator we cannot say which of the four funnels is weak or whether the problem is traffic, copy, placement, or offer. Every conversion directive stays guesswork until this exists.
- Definition of done: A pageview endpoint and table persist views server-side, privacy-friendly, no popups, no consent theater. The tracker mounts on `blog/[slug]`, the homepage, and all four funnel pages (`/sell/your-home`, `/sell/investment-property`, `/invest/deploy-capital`, `/rent/find-a-home`). `kpi-report.mjs` gains a traffic section: total pageviews, top 10 articles, per-funnel-page views, and view-to-lead rate. Next Monday's snapshot shows non-null traffic numbers.

### P2 — Finish Telegram alerts and make the daily pipeline fail loudly (carried from 2026-08-14 P2)
- Why: Principle 23 (never sacrifice long-term trust for quick cash): publish-live only protects trust if we see problems the same hour. KPI-adjacent gap: the cloud routine ran without `DATABASE_URL` on 5 of 7 days, today's article is still unpublished, and no alert fired. The lead-alert half is already built; the failure-alert half is the part that would have saved this week.
- Definition of done: `publish-article.mjs` sends a Telegram message per published article (title + live link). The daily routine sends a Telegram alert on any hard failure: missing `DATABASE_URL`, publish error, market-data refresh error, or a failed hero audit. The weekly KPI summary posts to Telegram. The routine's own configuration is fixed so `DATABASE_URL` is present every run, and the stranded Aug 17 article publishes. Owner action stays flagged: create the bot via BotFather and hand the CTO `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` and `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID`; nothing can fire without them. Email drafts stop once Telegram delivery is confirmed.

### P3 — Same-day distribution copy on every publish, and hold heroes at zero broken (carried from 2026-08-14 P3)
- Why: Principle 20 (strong marketing beats a strong product with no message) and principle 16 (a loyal customer is worth more than any funnel). Principle 26 (good visuals sell) backs the hero clause: 1 of 74 live articles still shows a broken hero today.
- Definition of done: On every publish day the pipeline writes ready-to-post distribution copy to `content/<channel>/<date>.md` for at least two channels, same day, for 7 consecutive days. Subscriber count moves off 1 within two weeks of P1 and P3 both running; if it does not, next report diagnoses with the new traffic data instead of guessing.

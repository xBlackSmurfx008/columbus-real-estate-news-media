### 1. The Guild Opens in Weinland Park: 260 Apartments, 78 Affordable, Targeting 2026

- **What**: Marker Development's 365,000-sq-ft mixed-use project at 303 E. 6th Ave. in Weinland Park is on track for a 2026 opening, bringing 260 apartments — 78 priced at 80% AMI — and 3,400 sq ft of ground-floor retail to the East 5th corridor.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: Weinland Park sits between Italian Village and the OSU campus, a corridor investors have watched for years; this is the largest mixed-income project to land there, and its PACE + Capital Lease financing structure is a replicable playbook for affordable-inclusive deals in supply-constrained inner-ring neighborhoods.
- **Source(s)**: [Columbus-Franklin County Finance Authority](https://columbusfinance.org/news/the-guild-development-honors-the-past-and-brings-260-apartment-homes-to-weinland-park/) (canonical), [Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/05/east-5th-shakeup-260-new-apartments-rise-by-italian-village/), [Marker Development](https://buildwithmarker.com/our-work-in-weinland-park/)

### 2. Brewery District Gets 106-Unit Apartment After Former WWCD Radio Building Demolished

### 3. $90M Water & Light District Breaks Ground in Marysville with Prefab Apartments

- **What**: Connect Real Estate has broken ground on the $90M Water and Light District — an 18-acre mixed-use project on the site of Marysville's historic 1891 water treatment plant — that will include approximately 300 units (market rate, senior, townhomes) plus retail and trails. Phase one apartments are being prefabricated in a Columbus warehouse by Connect Housing Blocks and will be assembled on-site this fall.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: Marysville is an active Columbus-metro growth market and this is the most significant private investment into its urban core in decades; the prefab construction method is a cost model Columbus-based operators can watch as a proof of concept for accelerating delivery timelines.
- **Source(s)**: [Columbus Underground](https://columbusunderground.com/construction-begins-at-the-water-and-light-district-in-marysville-we1/) (canonical), [ABC6/WSYX](https://abc6onyourside.com/news/local/water-and-light-district-and-the-silos-marysville-plant-demolition-set-next-month-as-90m-redevelopment-project-kicks-off), [Marysville Journal Tribune](https://www.marysvillejt.com/2026/03/20/demo-former-water-treatment-plant-underway/), [Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/04/marysville-s-big-bet-uptown-makeover-silo-nightlife-and-a-creekside-comeback/)

### LinkedIn

**One of Columbus' most-watched neighborhoods just got its biggest new project.**

Marker Development's *The Guild* — a 260-unit, five-story mixed-use building at 303 E. 6th Ave. in Weinland Park — is on track to open in 2026, becoming the largest residential addition to the East 5th corridor in recent years.

Here's what the deal looks like for Columbus operators:

**78 of 260 units priced at 80% AMI** — a 30% affordable set-aside in a market-rate-dominant neighborhood, achieved without a conventional public subsidy stack.

**The financing is the story.** Marker layered the Columbus-Franklin County Finance Authority's Capital Lease program with Commercial Property Assessed Clean Energy (PACE) financing. Capital Lease lowers construction costs via tax treatment during the build; PACE funds energy-efficiency improvements through a property tax assessment, freeing conventional debt capacity during lease-up. Together they created the headroom to price a third of the units below market — a structure other Columbus developers are watching closely.

**The community process matters too.** The project took years of engagement with the Weinland Park Community Civic Association, and the developer honored that history by naming the project after the Godman Guild Association, the longtime social service nonprofit that originally held the site.

Weinland Park sits between the Short North, Italian Village, and the OSU campus. It has been under-supplied relative to demand for years. The Guild, combined with a 186-unit project at 200 E. 5th Ave. and the Thrive Companies' 4th & 5th mixed-use redevelopment nearby, will change that supply calculus by end of 2026.

For Columbus developers watching affordable-inclusive economics: the Capital Lease + PACE stack is worth a closer look.

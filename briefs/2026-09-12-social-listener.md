#### 1. $500M affordable housing bond — momentum vs. accountability gap — STEADY | MIXED/SKEPTICAL | **CONTENT OPPORTUNITY**

Mayor Ginther's $500M affordable housing bond (approved by Columbus voters November 2025 as part of a $1.9B bond package) has a published deployment plan — $150M for building/preserving units, $125M for homelessness capital, $175M for land acquisitions, $50M for innovative construction approaches — but community watchdogs are asking where the accountability mechanisms are.

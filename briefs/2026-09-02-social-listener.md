**3. Zone In zoning reform — neighborhood-vs-city rift widening**
- Volume: **steady** (sustained coverage across WOSU, Columbus.gov, Planetizen, Yahoo since Dec 2025)
- Sentiment: **mixed / frustrated** (neighbors skeptical; pro-density voices supportive)
- Frame: **threat + content opportunity (community explainer)**

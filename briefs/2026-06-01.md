### Columbus's $430M Merchant Building Approaches Q4 2026 Opening

- **What**: The Merchant Building — a 32-story mixed-use tower at Columbus's historic North Market — is tracking toward a Q4 2026 completion. Developed by Columbus-based Rockbridge and Edwards Companies and designed by Seattle's NBBJ, the project has grown to $430M from its original $345M budget and will deliver 174 rental apartments, a 206-room boutique hotel, 65,000 sq ft of Class A office space, a 350-space parking garage, and a two-story, 15,000 sq ft expansion of North Market that will add eight to nine new vendors.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: At $430M, the Merchant Building is the single largest mixed-use development in Downtown Columbus in decades. Its apartment units will reset the high-water mark for Downtown rental comps; the office supply enters a market where 21 tenants are already searching 271,000 sq ft in the urban core; and the North Market expansion will intensify foot traffic for the entire North Market District — creating tailwinds for operators and ground-floor retail tenants from Short North south to Scioto Peninsula.
- **Source(s)**: [Merchant Building: 174 units — Construction Dive](https://www.constructiondive.com/news/merchant-building-downtown-columbus-north-market/718876/) (canonical), [Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/merchant-building-downtown-columbus-north-market/719566/), [Smart Cities Dive](https://www.smartcitiesdive.com/news/merchant-building-residential-downtown-columbus-north-market/719640/), [Columbus Underground — construction timeline](https://columbusunderground.com/new-name-construction-timeline-unveiled-for-north-market-tower-bw1/), [NBC4 — 2026 projects to watch](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/central-ohio-news/top-central-ohio-construction-projects-to-watch-in-2026/)

### Peninsula Phase II Lands Giant Eagle Market District at Downtown Columbus

- **What**: The Daimler Group and Flaherty & Collins Properties are building a $211M, 14-story mixed-use building at the corner of Belle and Broad Streets in downtown Columbus. Giant Eagle will operate a 20,000 sq ft Market District grocery on the ground floor; 250 apartments rise above, with an attached parking garage. Construction expected to begin in 2026; completion targeted 2027–2028.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: Downtown Columbus has lacked a full-service grocery for years — a barrier that operators and city planners have consistently cited as a drag on residential absorption. Giant Eagle anchoring The Peninsula Phase II signals that Downtown has crossed the residential-density threshold that national grocery chains require. This is the amenity unlock that accelerates absorption for every apartment operator between COSI and Nationwide Arena.
- **Source(s)**: [10TV — Giant Eagle / Peninsula Phase 2](https://www.10tv.com/article/news/local/boomtown-ohio/the-peninsula-phase-2-giant-eagle-downtown-columbus/530-c2b7fff7-fdcc-481f-bc24-cb0d8b9319d3) (canonical), [Flaherty & Collins press release](https://flco.com/flaherty-collins-properties-announces-plans-for-211m-phase-two-at-the-peninsula-in-downtown-columbus/), [ABC6](https://abc6onyourside.com/news/local/giant-eagle-to-open-new-market-district-in-downtown-columbus-belle-broad-streets-bob-white-jr-daimlere-group-mayor-andrew-ginther-the-penninsula), [Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2025/09/giant-eagle-to-open-new-market-district-store-in-downtown-columbus-s-peninsula-development/)

### Zone In Phase II Reshapes Development Rules for 43% of Columbus Parcels

- **What**: Columbus City Council approved Phase II of the city's Zone In modernization, rezoning approximately 66,000 properties — 43% of the city's total parcels — with the goal of enabling 200,000 additional housing units over the next decade. Phase III, targeting single-family residential districts, is not expected until 2027.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: Phase II unlocks density on corridors and transitional zones where previously only low-intensity uses were permitted. Developers and investors holding or acquiring sites along East Main, West Broad, and Northwest corridors need to revisit their highest-and-best-use assumptions now — allowed building heights and use mixes have materially changed.
- **Source(s)**: [10TV — City Council vote](https://www.10tv.com/article/news/local/columbus-city-council-approves-phase-two-zone-in-initiative/530-777ab85d-46d5-4974-89fa-ad20ee8f27fd) (canonical), [Columbus Underground — Phase II scope](https://columbusunderground.com/next-phase-of-zoning-reform-will-cover-43-of-city-bw1/), [City of Columbus announcement](https://www.columbus.gov/News-articles/City-of-Columbus-Proposes-to-Expand-Reach-and-Focus-of-Zoning-Code-Modernization)

### The Guild Brings 260 Apartments — 78 Affordable — to Weinland Park

- **What**: Marker Development's The Guild is a 365,000 sq ft mixed-use project adding 260 urban infill apartments to Weinland Park, with 78 units priced for households earning up to 80% of Area Median Income. Marker Construction is managing the build. Project financing involved the Columbus-Franklin County Finance Authority.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: Weinland Park is one of Columbus's most closely watched displacement-pressure neighborhoods. Marker's mixed-income approach — threading market-rate and affordable units in the same project — is the template that city planners are pushing as an alternative to standalone affordable-only buildings, and how it leases up will shape the next round of subsidy negotiations citywide.
- **Source(s)**: [Columbus-Franklin County Finance Authority — The Guild](https://columbusfinance.org/news/the-guild-development-honors-the-past-and-brings-260-apartment-homes-to-weinland-park/) (canonical)

### Columbus Rental Market Softens Slightly as Supply Deliveries Approach

- **What**: Average Columbus apartment rent reached $1,425/mo as of May 16, 2026 — 27% below the national average and down approximately 2% year-over-year. One-bedrooms average $1,202/mo; studios average $982/mo. The market's largest share (56%) of units falls in the $1,001–$1,500/mo band.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: With the Merchant Building's 174 units, GreenHouse Franklinton's 313 units (Gravity Phase III), and several other deliveries in H2 2026, rent pressure is expected to remain flat-to-soft. Operators underwriting 2026 acquisitions or refinances should model conservative growth — Columbus's affordability relative to peer markets remains a demand anchor, but near-term supply headwinds are real.
- **Source(s)**: [Zumper — Columbus rent research](https://www.zumper.com/rent-research/columbus-oh), [RentCafe — Columbus trends](https://www.rentcafe.com/average-rent-market-trends/us/oh/columbus/), [Point2Homes — Columbus avg rent](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Average-Rent/OH/Columbus.html)

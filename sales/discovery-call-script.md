# Discovery Call Script

## Opening

Thanks for taking the call. The goal today is to understand your target audience and map a local campaign that aligns with your growth goals.

## Questions

1. Which Columbus areas matter most for your business?
2. Which customer type is highest value for you right now?
3. What is your current lead source mix?
4. What campaign timeline and budget are you targeting?
5. What would success look like in 90 days?

## Recommendation Structure

- Suggested placements by area and topic
- Estimated visibility and engagement indicators
- 90-day package recommendation
- Launch timeline and reporting cadence

## Close

If this plan looks aligned, I will send the proposal and insertion details by end of day.

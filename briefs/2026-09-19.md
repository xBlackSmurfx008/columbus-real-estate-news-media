### LinkedIn (~1,450 chars)

Zone In Phase 2 is Columbus's biggest zoning move since Phase 1 — and the public comment window is open right now, through October 24.

The city released the draft on August 20. It covers 70,000+ parcels — about 41% of the city's land — under nine new zoning districts across three categories:

→ **Mixed-use corridors:** Additional streets where housing + neighborhood retail can proceed by-right, without a discretionary hearing
→ **Industrial/warehouse:** Updated districts for Columbus's east, south, and west industrial land; clearer separation of uses as conversion pressure grows
→ **Campus edges:** New districts for the perimeters of OSU, Nationwide Children's Hospital, and OhioHealth — the high-demand edges that Phase 1 didn't specifically address

If Phase 1 opened up High Street and downtown to by-right development, Phase 2 does something similar for a much larger share of the city.

For operators: the map is live at zone-in-columbus.hub.arcgis.com — worth checking against any site outside the downtown/High Street core. The final council vote is expected before year-end, but the draft can still change. Build the final ordinance into your watch list, not the draft.

To comment before October 24: use the survey at the Zone In site, or visit the walk-in gallery at 141 N. Front St. (weekdays 11am–2pm and 4–6pm, Saturdays 11am–1pm). Email: zoningupdate@columbus.gov.

Full story at Columbus Real Estate News.

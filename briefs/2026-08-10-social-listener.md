**3. RealPage Settlement — Objection Deadline is September 1, Not January 2027**  
Volume: **STEADY** | Sentiment: **Negative / Frustrated / Curious**  
Frame: **Threat + Content idea** — The Aug 9 brief established that Columbus renters can file claims through January 29, 2027. Today's scan surfaces a separate, much sooner deadline: class members who want to object to or opt out of the $359.9M settlement must do so by **September 1, 2026 — 22 days from today**. This two-deadline structure is not being communicated clearly in any Columbus-local outlet. Most tenants who find the story assume January 2027 is their only date to act.

The final approval hearing is October 15, 2026. The claim deadline is January 29, 2027. But opting out or objecting — the rights that allow tenants to preserve independent legal action against named landlords — requires action by September 1.

Columbus landlords named in the DOJ suit: Greystar Real Estate Partners, Blackstone's LivCor, Camden Property Trust, Cushman & Wakefield/Pinnacle, Willow Bridge Property Company, and Cortland Management. CW Columbus confirmed at least one Columbus landlord already struck a separate deal with the DOJ.

**4. Ohio Brownfield Remediation — $120M Opens August 11 (Tomorrow)**  
Volume: **LIGHT** | Sentiment: **Opportunity / Curious**  
Frame: **Opportunity + Content idea** — Applications for the latest round of Ohio's Brownfield Remediation Program open **tomorrow, August 11**, and run through September 11. The round makes $120M available for cleanup of contaminated industrial, commercial, and institutional properties statewide. Since 2021 the program has awarded nearly $800M across 904 projects in 87 counties — and Columbus-area brownfield sites are directly eligible. This is a developer/municipal audience story with strong real estate angle: brownfield cleanup unlocks development sites, often in infill locations prime for housing or mixed-use.

Per WCSM Radio and GrantedAI, the application period runs 10 a.m. Aug. 11 to 5 p.m. Sept. 11. Ohio Department of Development is administering. $1M per county is reserved for fiscal year 2026, with the balance awarded on merit.

Volume: LIGHT — confirmed via Rep. King press release summary (WCSM Radio), GrantedAI grant database, and Ohio EPA program pages. Community conversation minimal but developer/government audience is the primary stakeholder; awareness of the program opening tomorrow is low.

Sources: WCSM Radio (wcsmradio.com, confirmed via search), GrantedAI (grantedai.com, confirmed), Ohio EPA (epa.ohio.gov/divisions-and-offices/environmental-response-revitalization/derr-programs/ohio-brownfields, confirmed), Ohio Department of Development (confirmed)

### 3. M/I Homes Withdraws 500+ Home Licking County Development — Regional Monitor

**Coverage signal:** Monitor — regional story, outside Franklin County core. Not yet covered.
**Confidence:** 4/5
**What:** M/I Homes withdrew its zoning application and development agreement to build more than 500 homes on a nearly 300-acre site in the City of Heath (Licking County), near State Route 37, as of July 31. The withdrawal followed significant opposition from Heath and Granville residents (Granville schools would have served the development, raising district capacity and financial concerns). Heath City Council had been scheduled to vote on the proposal within days.
**Why it matters:** Heath is in the Columbus metro area. M/I Homes is one of central Ohio's largest volume homebuilders. The withdrawal is a high-profile example of greenfield suburban development being stopped by community opposition and school-district resource concerns — relevant to Columbus metro housing supply. Separately, the collapse is reported to have prompted an annexation law push in Ohio (CW Columbus), which could affect suburban growth rules metro-wide.

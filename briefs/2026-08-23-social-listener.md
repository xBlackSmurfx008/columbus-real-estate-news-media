**Summary:** The City of Columbus released the formal Phase 2 Zone In draft on August 20, 2026. ### 4. ADU momentum — contractor "how-to" content proliferating 8 months post-legalization — STEADY, curious/optimistic

**Summary:** Columbus City Council legalized ADUs in all residential districts effective December 24, 2025. Eight months later, search results show a growing ecosystem of contractor and real estate how-to content targeting Columbus homeowners: Compton Construction, JS Brown Company, Steadily, ColumbusForward (Otto Beatty initiative), and the Planetizen/Columbus Underground coverage of the ordinance. **Volume signal:** Steady — multiple distinct contractor and advisory firms publishing Columbus-specific ADU guides in 2026; not a one-day spike but a sustained search-interest signal.

**Sentiment:** Curious / optimistic among homeowners; some skepticism about construction costs and contractor availability.

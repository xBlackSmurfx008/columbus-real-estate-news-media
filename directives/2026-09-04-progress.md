whole defect. All three are now live, sourced, gate-passing 18/18, with source,
claim, and entity ledgers. **None needed unpublishing.** But the repair found:

  the sale was made "so the site can continue serving the community in a

  to Columbus State president David Harrison a statement that "food insecurity
  is a leading non-academic barrier to student success" — in no fetched source.
  Both removed. Verified gone from production.
- **A factually wrong headline.** "Former Garver YMCA site **sold** to CMHA" —
  the YMCA had only *agreed to sell*; no closed sale is on the record. Now
  "CMHA **Seeks** 216 Affordable Apartments at Closed Garver YMCA Site."

  appeared in city records," when CMHA had already presented to the Greater
  South East Area Commission and requested variances.

  February, and was stale by August.
- **Invented arithmetic** — "a roughly 18-month build once ground breaks,"
  derived from a spring 2027 start and an unspecified 2028 date.
- **A sourcing claim in a meta description** — "county records show," when no
  county record had been fetched.

  62.67 → 62.666 as the city lists it.

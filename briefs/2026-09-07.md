Second Baptist Church — a Near East Side institution marking its 190th year — is opening 2.4 acres of Columbus land to development partners.

The church posted an RFP this week for five sites in the Near East Side, with the largest being a 1.17-acre contiguous block fronting East Long Street between 17th and 18th Streets. That's a meaningful amount of ground in a neighborhood where buildable lots are typically small and assembling multiple parcels is costly and slow.

The church isn't new to this. Its prior housing effort, Chandler Arms, delivered 45 units of senior housing. This time, the scope is wider — and the RFP is open to partnership structures beyond a simple sale.

What form does the project take? That depends on what Second Baptist decides to accept. A LIHTC-backed affordable deal would provide income-restricted units but move slower; a market-rate project moves faster but delivers less on neighborhood affordability goals.

Details and submission instructions are at eastpact.org/rfp. The deadline was not publicly confirmed at our publication time.

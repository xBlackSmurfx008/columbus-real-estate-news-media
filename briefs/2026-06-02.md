### Hikma Commits $216M to Columbus Pharma Campus, Adds Global R&D Hub

- **What**: Hikma Pharmaceuticals USA announced a $216 million investment in its Columbus manufacturing facility at 1809 Wilson Road on June 1, 2026, expanding oral solid dose and nasal inhalation manufacturing at the company's 1-million-square-foot, 80-acre campus on the city's West Side. The announcement simultaneously elevated Columbus to one of Hikma's three global R&D hubs. The Columbus campus is already the largest single-site manufacturing employer in the City of Columbus, with more than 1,200 employees. The Columbus investment is part of a $267 million dual-site Ohio expansion that also includes $51 million for injectable pharma manufacturing in Bedford, and is a component of Hikma's $1 billion U.S. manufacturing and R&D expansion announced in 2025.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: For Columbus industrial operators and investors, this is the largest announced manufacturing reinvestment in the West Side corridor in recent memory. The R&D hub designation signals that Hikma's Columbus presence is transitioning from a pure production anchor to a higher-value global hub — attracting research talent and institutional capital that strengthens the entire central Ohio life sciences cluster. Industrial investors monitoring the West Side should treat this as a leading indicator for the corridor's long-term absorption and facility value.
- **Source(s)**: [Hikma Announces Major Ohio Expansion — GlobeNewswire, June 1 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/01/3304631/0/en/Hikma-Pharmaceuticals-Announces-Major-Expansion-in-Ohio-Creating-350-New-Jobs-and-Investing-267-Million.html) (canonical), [JobsOhio press release](https://www.jobsohio.com/newsroom/news-press/hikma-pharmaceuticals-announces-major-expansion-in-ohio-creating-350-new-jobs-and-investing-267-million), [Plant Services — $1B U.S. investment](https://www.plantservices.com/industry-news/news/55300553/hikma-pharmaceuticals-invests-1b-to-expand-pharmaceutical-manufacturing-and-rd-facilities-in-ohio-and-new-jersey), [Hikma U.S. Groundbreaking — Accessible Medicines](https://accessiblemeds.org/resources/press-releases/hikma-us-expansion-one-billion-investment/), [Ohio Life Sciences profile](https://ohiolifesciences.org/business-directory/115225/hikma-pharmaceuticals-usa-inc/)

### Capital Line Stage 3 Begins: Gay Street Streetscape Construction Underway

- **What**: The Capital Line's Stage 3 — the full surface reconstruction phase — began in June 2026, bringing visible streetscape overhaul to Gay Street in downtown Columbus. Each block between Fourth Street and Front Street will close to vehicles for three to four months at a time while crews resurface the roadway and install new paving, landscaping, benches, and lighting. Stage 3 runs through October 2027; the full Gay Street redesign is expected to be complete by end of 2026 or early 2027. The $100 million total project, led by Downtown Columbus Inc. and the City of Columbus, will deliver a two-mile physically separated pedestrian and bike corridor connecting the Gay Street District to the Scioto Peninsula. The City launched the Capital Line Business Rent Support Grant to offset impacts on affected operators.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: Gay Street is a primary commercial and retail corridor. Rolling vehicle closures will redirect parking and foot traffic for months at a stretch, directly affecting ground-floor retail and restaurant operators with leases on and near the corridor. The Business Rent Support Grant is actionable now. When Stage 3 completes, the Capital Line will function as a competitive amenity upgrade for every property fronting or adjacent to Gay Street — the kind of public infrastructure investment that typically reprices rents upward as it matures.
- **Source(s)**: [Construction Starts on $100M Capital Line — NBC4](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/construction-starts-on-100-million-capital-line-project-in-downtown-columbus/) (canonical), [City Offers Help to Businesses — NBC4](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/city-offers-help-to-businesses-affected-by-downtown-capital-line-construction/), [Capital Line Project Page — Downtown Columbus](https://downtowncolumbus.com/projects-initiatives/capitalline/), [New Capital Line Renderings — Columbus Underground](https://columbusunderground.com/new-capital-line-renderings-released-ahead-of-public-meeting-bw1/) (confirmed via search summary)

### VanTrust New Albany 300: 300,400-SF Spec Building Fully Leased at June 2026 Delivery

- **What**: VanTrust Real Estate's New Albany 300 — a 300,400-square-foot speculative industrial building at 12550 Jug St NW in the New Albany International Business Park — is fully leased ahead of its June 2026 delivery date. The building features 36-foot clear heights and a 15-year 100% tax abatement. VanTrust has started construction on its seventh speculative industrial project in New Albany (302,400 SF at the same location), its tenth overall in the park, continuing a pattern of back-to-back spec starts triggered by rapid lease-up. The park sits adjacent to Intel's planned semiconductor campus in Licking County.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: Pre-delivery full lease-up on a 300,400-SF spec building signals that New Albany's industrial market is absorbing supply faster than delivery — the condition that drives developers to start the next spec project before the prior one opens. VanTrust's 7th spec start confirms they believe demand is durable enough to justify another 302,000 SF. For industrial investors and land holders in the New Albany / Licking County corridor, this is the most actionable piece of market evidence this week.
- **Source(s)**: [VanTrust Columbus Development and Leasing Momentum — Columbus Chamber](https://columbus.org/vantrust-columbus-continues-strong-development-and-leasing-momentum-vantrust-real-estate/) (canonical), [New Albany 300 Property Page — VanTrust](https://vantrustrealestate.com/property/new-albany-300/), [VanTrust Seventh Spec Start — ConnectCRE](https://www.connectcre.com/stories/vantrust-to-start-seventh-speculative-project-in-ohio/), [New Albany Warehouse Snapped Up — Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/04/new-albany-warehouse-snapped-up-before-it-even-opens-as-vantrust-races-to-build-more/) (confirmed via search summary)

### Franklin County Municipal Courthouse: $300M Demolition Underway, Construction November 2026

- **What**: Franklin County is clearing the site at 410 S. High Street for a new $300 million, eight-story municipal courthouse after a multi-year delay. Demolition of the James A. Karnes Building began in March 2026; the adjacent Dorrian Commons Park has been cleared. Construction on the new courthouse is scheduled to start in November 2026, targeting a 2030 completion. Columbus-based Moody Nolan is leading design. A sky bridge will connect the new building to the Franklin County Government Center across South High Street. The project was funded by a 2019 voter-approved $130 million bond issue, with the budget subsequently increased due to construction cost inflation.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: A $300M civic anchor on South High Street will bring multi-year construction activity and ultimately a large daily user base to a block that has underperformed. For office and retail operators between Columbus Commons and the Brewery District, the courthouse is a long-term foot-traffic driver — but also a construction disruption through 2030 that complicates near-term leasing and absorption on the corridor.
- **Source(s)**: [Columbus Targets 2030 Completion for New Courthouse — NBC4](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/columbus-targets-2030-completion-for-new-300-million-downtown-courthouse/), [Columbus Advances Municipal Courthouse — Columbus Today](https://nationaltoday.com/us/oh/columbus/news/2026/04/03/columbus-advances-new-municipal-courthouse-project/), [Demolition for New Courthouse — Columbus Underground](https://columbusunderground.com/demolition-of-downtown-building-park-to-make-way-for-new-courthouse-bw1/) (confirmed via search summary)

### Brewery District: WWCD Site Approved for 106-Unit Arcadia Development

- **What**: The Historic Resources Commission voted 5-2 to approve demolition of the former WWCD radio building — a 1962 Swiss Chalet-style structure at 1036 S. Front St in the Brewery District — and the design of a replacement six-story apartment building from Arcadia Development. The 106-unit building will include a second-floor pool deck and a 51-car first-floor parking garage. Approval came with conditions requiring Arcadia to submit additional plans for lighting and signage for final staff sign-off.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: The Brewery District's South Front Street corridor continues to absorb urban infill residential. Arcadia's 106-unit project adds market-rate density to a block currently occupied by a single-story structure and surface parking, incrementally tightening the corridor between the Scioto Peninsula and the German Village edge. The 5-2 vote with conditions is a normal HRC outcome — but worth monitoring for any design appeal before permits are issued.

### Columbus State's $60M OCAT Advanced Technologies Hub Enters Design Phase

- **What**: Columbus State Community College's Ohio Center for Advanced Technologies (OCAT) has secured its final major funding tranche — $9.5 million in federal appropriations approved in February 2026 — bringing the total project budget to approximately $60 million ($9.5M federal + $20M state + $30M local bond). The 100,000-square-foot building will be constructed at the southeast corner of Cleveland Avenue and E. Spring Street. Moody Nolan is designing; Daimler Group is construction manager at risk. Design work is expected to begin in the first half of 2026. OCAT will house IT, advanced manufacturing, and biotech training and is part of a six-acre Columbus State parking lot redevelopment that also includes a new campus YMCA.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: OCAT's six-acre site redevelopment converts underutilized parking along Cleveland Avenue into a dense civic and educational cluster. The Daimler Group's role as CM@R connects this to other major Columbus CRE developments — Daimler is also co-developing The Peninsula Phase II Downtown. OCAT's workforce training focus in advanced manufacturing and biotech feeds directly into Intel's New Albany supply chain demand and Columbus's growing life sciences employer base, including today's Hikma expansion.
- **Source(s)**: [Congress Approves $9.5M for OCAT — Columbus State](https://www.cscc.edu/about/news/2026/OCAT.shtml), [Columbus State $9.5M Hub — Spectrum News 1](https://spectrumnews1.com/oh/columbus/news/2026/02/05/columbus-state-gets--9-5-million-approved-for-new-hub), [Columbus State's $60M Hub Hits Funding Milestone — Ohio Tech News](https://www.ohiotechnews.com/columbus-states-60-million-advanced-manufacturing-hub-hits-funding-milestone/)

## This run's material finding: the image supply is a *structural* blocker, not a one-day miss

Redistributable, documentary photographs **do exist** on Wikimedia Commons — but only for **existing,
well-known Columbus places**:

- **North Market:** 15 images returned, all under redistributable licenses (CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain).
- **Scioto Peninsula / Scioto Mile:** multiple CC BY / CC BY-SA / public-domain (NARA) images.
- **LeVeque Tower:** documentary photos of the tower itself exist under open licenses.

The problem is a **mismatch between what is newsworthy now and what is openly photographed.** Today's
current real-estate subjects are **new construction, proposals, or zoning-code corridors** — none of which
has a rights-cleared documentary photograph of its *actual* subject. The openly-photographed landmarks,
meanwhile, have **no current news hook**. Forcing an existing-landmark photo onto a new-project story
(for example, a North Market hall photo on a story about the adjacent Merchant tower) would fail
`story_match` and mislead the reader, so it is barred.

**Owner-actionable levers to unblock publication:** (a) supply CREN-owned or commissioned site
photography for a verified current lead; or (b) prioritize stories whose subject is an existing,
openly-photographed landmark that also has a genuine current news hook. Paid/AI generation and stock
placeholders remain barred in this environment.

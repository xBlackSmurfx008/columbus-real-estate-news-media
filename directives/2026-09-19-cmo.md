### P1 — Fix the unreachable placeholder path in `publish-article.mjs` so an article can go live with `DATABASE_URL` alone, then ship the five stranded drafts
- Why: KPI gap — Articles published in window fell to 1, pageviews fell
  78 → 68, and no article has published since Sep 12 while all four funnels
  sit at 0 views, making content the only compounding asset the property has.
  The cause is now located in repository code, not in a missing credential:
  `frontend/scripts/publish-article.mjs` INSERTs the row with `status='live'`
  and `image_url` NULL (around line 254), and only afterwards attempts to
  attach a branded editorial card (lines 289–309), gated on
  `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`. The database CHECK constraint
  `articles_live_image_required` rejects that INSERT first, so the placeholder
  fallback is unreachable for every imageless article in every environment.
  The Sep 17 second-run session proves this: it had `DATABASE_URL` and still
  could not publish a single article. This also contradicts the documented
  repo policy that images never block publication. Principle 11 (focus over
  tools) and principle 8 (find demand and position yourself): the Diocese
  story had confirmed, loud, negative public demand and a finished draft, and
  the property still shipped nothing. This outranks P2 and P3 because it is
  the only binding constraint fully inside the CTO's control, and it gates the
  property's sole compounding asset.
- Definition of done: (a) `publish-article.mjs` resolves a durable `https://`
  hero URL **before** the INSERT — either the supplied image or a branded
  placeholder that does not require `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` — so that
  `node scripts/publish-article.mjs <file.json>` succeeds in an environment
  where only `DATABASE_URL` is set, proven by one stranded draft publishing
  from such a session; (b) the chosen image rule is documented in the repo so
  it and the `articles_live_image_required` constraint no longer contradict
  each other; (c) `DATABASE_URL` is restored to the AM routine, proven by one
  AM brief recording successful `recent-articles.mjs` and `coverage-calendar`
  runs; (d) all five stranded drafts are each live or spiked with a recorded
  reason — Diocese reframed as a Sep 22 hearing result rather than an advance,
  plus OSU/Moritz, Broad & Gould, Italian Village Merus, and Mercy on Main;
  (e) the Zone In Phase 2 council-vote status is verified against columbus.gov
  council records, and either a follow-up article publishes or the existing
  top-traffic explainer carries a dated verification note, because that page
  is the property's highest-traffic article (8 views) and may now describe a
  vote that has already happened; (f) the next KPI report shows Articles
  published in window of 4 or more.

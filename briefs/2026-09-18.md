### LinkedIn (1,312 chars)

A Cincinnati developer with Columbus roots is bringing 65 affordable apartments to Olde Towne East — backed by a 2026 Ohio Housing Finance Agency tax credit award.

The project is called Mercy on Main, planned for 907–917 E. Main St. Developer Kingsley + Co. was founded by Chinedum Ndukwe, a former Cincinnati Bengals and Oakland Raiders linebacker who grew up in central Ohio and retired from the NFL in 2012 to build below-market housing in Ohio cities.

The name comes from his grandmother, Mercy Okoronkwo. "She was extremely caring and generous and supportive, and a fearless individual," Ndukwe told NBC4.

A few things worth noting for operators and investors:

→ The $22.8M project includes 65 units: 8 studios, 29 one-BR, 18 two-BR, 10 three-BR — a mix that serves families, not just single renters
→ The site is on the planned East Main Street BRT corridor, near COTA's Near East Transit Center and Nationwide Children's Hospital
→ OHFA's 9% LIHTC program is highly competitive — a statewide $39.1M allocation with multiple projects competing
→ Tax credit award ≠ groundbreaking. Financing must still close before construction can start. Watch for permits at 907–917 E. Main St.
→ This is Kingsley + Co.'s first announced standalone Columbus project, though the company is also partnering with Borror Development on a King-Lincoln District site

Full story at Columbus Real Estate News.

## Sales Principles

- Sell attention and useful distribution, not editorial influence.
- Use only verified audience numbers in media kits.
- Keep every paid placement labeled.
- Require claim substantiation for objective claims.
- Separate newsroom, sales, branded content, and legal review.
- Start managed, then automate the repeatable parts.
- Do not sell lead guarantees, lease guarantees, closing guarantees, ranking guarantees, or editorial coverage.
- Do not launch referral-fee, lending, insurance, brokerage, or investment/capital compensation without counsel.

## Advertiser Intake Checklist

Collect before quoting:

- Legal advertiser name.
- Public brand/display name.
- Website and landing page.
- Business category and geography.
- Campaign goal.
- Desired audience: renters, buyers, sellers, owners, investors, agents, developers, PMs, local residents.
- Product interest.
- Budget range.
- Preferred dates.
- Creative assets available.
- Required claims and offers.
- Licenses, registrations, insurance, awards, testimonials, or guarantees mentioned.
- Fair-housing relevance.
- Lead routing requested: yes/no.
- Compensation model requested: flat ad fee, sponsored profile, affiliate, referral fee, pay per lead, pay per lease, success fee.
- Compliance contact.

## Claims Review Checklist

Require substantiation before approval for:

- Prices, rates, fees, discounts, concessions, rent ranges, availability.
- "Free", "no fee", "guaranteed", "best", "top", "lowest", "highest", "exclusive", "limited time".
- Lease-up, occupancy, conversion, lead-quality, return-on-ad-spend, or performance claims.
- License, insurance, certification, award, rating, review, or years-in-business claims.
- Before/after, testimonial, case-study, or consumer-result claims.
- AI/data accuracy claims.
- Investment return, appreciation, cash flow, tax, lending, insurance, or legal claims.

## Prohibited or Counsel-Gated Sales

Do not sell without counsel:

- Paid brokerage referrals.
- Lender referrals or mortgage rate leads.
- Insurance referrals.
- Investment/capital introductions.
- Success fees or transaction-based compensation.
- Pay-per-lease or pay-per-closing products.
- Guaranteed exclusive area/category sponsorship if it could mislead readers or restrict fair access.
- Audience targeting by protected class or protected-class proxy.
- Apartment ranking, "best apartment", "safest neighborhood", "best schools", or demographic claims without approved methodology and legal review.

### Advertiser Responsibilities

Advertiser represents that it:

- Has authority to buy the placement.
- Owns or has permission to use submitted copy, images, logos, videos, documents, and trademarks.
- Has substantiation for all objective claims.
- Will keep landing pages accurate and compliant.
- Will not submit unlawful, discriminatory, deceptive, infringing, defamatory, spam, malware, or unsafe content.
- Will comply with fair housing, consumer protection, privacy, intellectual property, email, telemarketing, lending, insurance, securities, and professional licensing laws that apply to its business.

### Publisher Rights

CREN may:

- Review, reject, pause, remove, relabel, or require edits to ads and sponsored content.
- Require proof for claims.
- Refuse categories or campaigns that create legal, editorial, privacy, fair-housing, brand-safety, reader-safety, or reputation risk.
- Make minor formatting changes for display, accessibility, mobile fit, and house style without changing claim substance.
- Correct or remove placements that fail policy review.

### Labels and Editorial Separation

- Paid placements must use the approved label for the format.
- Advertising does not buy editorial coverage, ranking, conclusions, recommendations, corrections outcomes, or source treatment.
- Sponsored content is handled through the branded-content workflow and does not represent independent newsroom coverage unless clearly stated and independently produced.

### Reporting

CREN will provide available reporting such as:

- Placement date(s).
- Screenshot or proof of placement where applicable.
- Page views, sends, opens where available, clicks, leads if applicable, and notes on methodology/limitations.
- CREN does not guarantee impressions, clicks, leads, leases, closings, sales, attendance, rankings, editorial coverage, or business results unless the final signed order expressly says otherwise.

### Cancellation, Refunds, and Make-Goods

Draft posture:

- Advertiser-caused late assets, unsupported claims, policy violations, or landing-page issues do not automatically create refunds.
- CREN-caused missed placements or material label/display errors may be eligible for a make-good or partial credit defined in the final order.
- Completed sponsored work is generally non-refundable unless the final order says otherwise.
- Refund and make-good terms require counsel review.

8. Payment/deposit confirmed if required.
9. Creative scheduled.
10. Placement goes live with label.
11. Screenshots/proof captured.
12. Report delivered.

## Launch Sequence

1. Use managed sales only.
2. Build verified media kit.
3. Create 3 to 5 founding sponsor packages.
4. Run first newsletter/display campaigns.
5. Deliver manual campaign reports.
6. Turn the best-performing package into repeatable product sheet.
7. Add advertiser account and campaign backend.
8. Add self-service asset intake.
9. Add online IO acceptance.
10. Add checkout only after refund, tax, accounting, and support workflows are ready.

- Product sheets exist for each sellable package.
- Advertising Terms and Sponsored Content Policy have counsel review before production.

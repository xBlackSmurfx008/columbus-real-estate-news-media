## What worked / what stalled

**Worked: the first real audience week in the site's history.** 119 pageviews
and 81 unique visitors, up from 2 pageviews last week (last week's directive
addendum). Subscribers went 1 → 9 (+8 in window), the first free member
arrived, and 5 contact messages came in. The traffic instrumentation directed
on 2026-08-17 and the cache/transfer fixes from 2026-08-24 are doing their job:
we can finally hear something (principle 17).

**Worked: timeliness beat authority.** The Sugar restaurant story — published
Aug 25, two days before the venue's Aug 27 opening — is the week's top page
at 19 views, ahead of the homepage (13). The COTA Line 30 service story took
10. Meanwhile the Columbus REALTORS July market report, our strongest data
story, is not in the top 10 at all. Readers came for "what is happening near
me this week," not market statistics. That is principle 8 in the data: demand
found us on timely local stories; position there.

**Worked: first affiliate activity ever recorded.** 6 clicks in the window
(renters-insurance 4, mortgage-rates 2) against 119 pageviews — roughly a 5%
click rate with no deliberate placement work behind it. Affiliate is revenue
lane #2 and it moved before lane #1 did.

**Stalled: lead intake — the #1 revenue lane — is at zero.** Real leads: 0.
The four funnel pages drew 3 total views out of 119 pageviews (FSBO 1,
capital 1, renter 1, investor-sale 0) — 2.5% of traffic. The bottleneck is no
longer traffic (81 people showed up) and cannot yet be judged as copy or offer:
almost nobody reaches the funnel pages to see the copy. This is placement.
Article pages are where readers are (top 10 pages = 7 articles), and articles
do not route readers to the funnel that matches the story they just read.

**Stalled: the publish pipeline dropped the week's best Neighborhoods story.**
Today's Mercy on Main article (Olde Towne East, 65 LIHTC units, OHFA award —
gate-passed 18/18 per the Aug 31 brief) is not in the database at all: the
daily routine ran without `DATABASE_URL` again. Briefs show the variable was
absent on Aug 25, 26, 27, and 31 — four of seven runs. The Aug 30 Northwest
Bank article did reach live (verified in the DB today, with a durable blob
hero), so the staged→auto-publish path works when the routine can reach the
database; the recurring failure is the routine's own configuration. "Articles
published in window: 9" happened despite this, not because it was fixed.
Principle 12: be valuable first — a story that never publishes has no value.

**Stalled (third consecutive week): no working alert channel.** Telegram
tokens still absent (`TELEGRAM_NOT_CONFIGURED` in the Aug 30 brief); the
uptime GitHub Action was failing at the billing/environment level per the
2026-08-24 progress file. Both silent failures this week (missing
`DATABASE_URL`, the Aug 30 draft-status scare) were caught by humans reading
briefs after the fact, not by an alert (principle 5: operating blind).

## Directives (prioritized)

### P1 — Make every gate-passed article reach the live site the same day
- Why: principle 21 (a killer offer won't save you in a dead market) and
  principle 12 (be valuable first). KPI gap: the routine ran without
  `DATABASE_URL` on 4 of 7 days; today's Neighborhoods story (Mercy on Main,
  18/18) is not in the database. The week's traffic (119 pageviews) came
  from published stories; every dropped story is dropped audience.
- What the CTO builds: (a) a catch-up publisher — an idempotent job (deploy
  hook, Vercel cron, or reconcile script) that finds gate-passing article
  JSONs in `content/blog/drafts/` absent from the `articles` table and runs
  them through `publish-article.mjs`, so a routine session without a database
  connection can commit the draft and the system self-heals; run it now to
  publish the stranded 2026-08-31 Mercy on Main article; (b) make the daily
  routine fail loudly at startup when `DATABASE_URL` is absent (push
  notification / GitHub issue, not just a brief line); (c) reconcile
  `CLAUDE.md` and the script header so the documented behavior matches the
  actual staged→auto-publish flow — the Aug 30 session lost a day to that
  contradiction. Owner dependency, flagged: add `DATABASE_URL` to the routine
  config at claude.ai/code/routines (asked in three consecutive directives).
- Definition of done: Mercy on Main is live at its `/blog/` URL; the catch-up
  job is on a schedule and its first run is logged in the progress file; a
  deliberate no-`DATABASE_URL` test run produces an alert a human sees; next
  Monday's brief-audit shows 0 days where a gate-passed article failed to
  reach the site.

### P2 — Route article readers to the matching funnel, and purge test leads so we can measure it
- Why: principles 8 (find demand and position yourself), 9 (they care what it
  does for them), 18/19 (about them, fast hook). KPI gap: 119 pageviews but
  3 funnel-page views (2.5%) and 0 real leads; the two recorded leads are
  `@example.com` test records that the smoke filter missed, making the 66.7%
  view-to-lead rate meaningless. Lead intake is revenue lane #1 and it is at
  zero while traffic finally exists.
- What the CTO builds: (a) delete or flag the two test lead rows and extend
  the KPI exclusion so `@example.com`/test-named records never count again —
  reporting stays honest (principle 23); (b) a contextual end-of-article CTA
  block mapped topic→funnel: renter/apartment/lifestyle stories →
  `/rent/find-a-home`, market-data and home-sale stories → `/sell/your-home`,
  investor/development stories → `/invest/deploy-capital` or
  `/sell/investment-property`. Inline block after the article body, "you"
  language, one sentence + one button. No popups, no interstitials — the
  owner's no-annoyance rule stands; (c) the block carries the existing
  funnel-page analytics tag so click-through is measured.
- Definition of done: test leads excluded from the next KPI run; every live
  article page renders exactly one topic-matched funnel block; next week's
  snapshot shows funnel-page views ≥ 10% of pageviews (12+ at this week's
  volume) and every one of the four funnels registers ≥ 1 view; view-to-lead
  is computed on real leads only.

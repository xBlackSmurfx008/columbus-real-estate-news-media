# Columbus Real Estate News Media - Media Kit

## Positioning

Local media brand focused on Columbus-area real estate and lifestyle topics, segmented by area and topic for targeted advertising.

## Audience

- Columbus residents
- Home buyers and sellers
- Investors and landlords
- Local professionals and service providers

## Audience Intelligence

Advertisers receive aggregate insights by:

- Area interest
- Topic interest
- Content engagement
- Newsletter segment performance

## Core Ad Products

- Display ads across homepage, hubs, and articles
- Sponsored content (articles/interviews/video)
- Newsletter sponsorship
- Featured local business directory profiles

## Why Advertise Here

- Hyperlocal audience intent
- Area-and-topic targeting
- Multi-format media placements
- First-party audience insights for campaign optimization

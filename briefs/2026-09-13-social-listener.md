#### 1. Zone In Phase 2 — developer vs. neighborhood control — STEADY | FRUSTRATED/DIVIDED | **CONTENT OPPORTUNITY**

The Zone In Phase 2 public comment period (60 days, through Oct. 24) is generating real community division. Multiple Columbus area commissions are refusing to endorse the citywide land-use map at the heart of Phase 2, which would shift zoning-map authority from the 21 individual area commissions to a single city-controlled map covering 43% of the city's land.

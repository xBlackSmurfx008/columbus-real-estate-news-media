### P1 — Establish a production deploy path and ship the conversion layer, proven by telemetry
- Why: KPI gap — 0 CTA clicks anywhere and 0 views on the three
  highest-priority funnels (FSBO, investor-sale, capital) across 131
  pageviews, the seventh consecutive snapshot with this exact reading, while
  search (49.6% of views — KPI snapshot) keeps delivering readers to pages
  with no conversion layer. Principles 13 (the offer isn't landing because
  it isn't placed), 19 (no hook is possible with no placement), and 9 (the
  reader never sees what CREN can do for them). The blocker is no longer a
  mystery: the Sep 11 Session 3 check (brief record) confirmed the merged
  conversion build is correct on `main`, production runs an older build, and
  no session has a deploy path. This outranks everything else because it
  gates every conversion metric on the property (leads, members, affiliate
  clicks), has shown zero movement in seven reviews while the other two
  constraints moved or held, and closes with one deploy-path action.
- Definition of done: (a) a durable deploy path exists and is documented —
  the Vercel project connected to the repo for deploy-on-push (dashboard →
  Settings → Git), or a deploy hook URL / `VERCEL_TOKEN` in the routine
  environment — proven by one production deploy of current `main`; (b) a
  live article URL fetched after the deploy renders the taxonomy-matched
  contextual CTA on the article template; (c) `verify:site -- --target
  production` no longer reports the `disclosure-funnel` blocking failures;
  (d) at least one flagged test `funnel_view` and `cta_click` from
  production pages appears in the KPI report's instrumentation, so the next
  snapshot can show nonzero rows the moment real readers see the CTAs.

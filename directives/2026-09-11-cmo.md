### P1 — Make the newsroom publish-capable and flush the stranded drafts
- Why: KPI gap — Articles published in window fell from 8 (2026-09-10
  snapshot) to 7 (today's snapshot), and content is the only measured growth
  channel: search sent 45.3% of this window's 137 pageviews (KPI snapshot).
  Operational record (briefs): eleven consecutive 06:33 runs without
  `DATABASE_URL`, no credentialed afternoon session on Sep 10 or Sep 11, and
  two stranded articles — the gate-passed Lower Olentangy Tunnel draft
  (pegged to an end-of-September completion) and the INTRO mass timber draft
  (topped out Sep 8; timeliness decays daily). Principle 8 (find demand and
  position yourself ahead of it) is impossible when the pipeline cannot
  publish, and the property's one measured traffic amplifier — publishing
  ahead of a dated peg (CLAUDE.md) — is exactly what stranding destroys.
  This outranks the conversion-layer work this week because the evaluation
  standard puts traffic and instrumentation ahead of conversion at 137
  pageviews, and because last week's P3 justification ("the workaround has
  kept cadence") is now falsified by two days of zero publishes.
- Definition of done: (a) `DATABASE_URL` is confirmed durably present in the
  routine's cloud configuration at claude.ai/code/routines (owner action,
  twelfth consecutive ask), verified by one 06:33 run that publishes its own
  gate-passed article; or, failing that, (b) a duplicate-safe `flush-drafts`
  step exists and runs in any credentialed session, automatically publishing
  gate-ready drafts through `publish-article.mjs`, so a blind AM run delays
  a story by hours, not days; and (c) the Sep 10 Lower Olentangy Tunnel
  draft, the Sep 11 INTRO mass timber draft, and each Sep 2–4 backlog draft
  get a recorded disposition (published via `publish-article.mjs` after the
  gate, or spiked per the stale-peg rule — never backdate).

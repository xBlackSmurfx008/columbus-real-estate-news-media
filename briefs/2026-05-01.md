### 1. Columbus Zone In: Detailed Phase 2 Standards Being Written — Operators Have a Window to Engage

- **What**: Columbus City Council adopted the Columbus Growth Strategy — the city's first-ever citywide land use policy and map — on January 27, 2026, completing a three-year Zone In overhaul of a code last updated in the 1950s. Zone In Phase 2 (passed 2025) rezoned 43% of the city's parcels (~66,000 properties) with six new mixed-use zone designations, eliminated parking minimums in those zones, and added bonus floors for affordable-unit inclusion. Detailed building-type, height, setback, and transition standards for Phase 2 are being developed throughout 2026 with public engagement ongoing; the Zone In gallery at 141 N. Front St. is the primary input venue. [June 10 comment deadline cited in multiple 2026 sources — verify before publish]
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: Every Columbus operator with sites on the 12,300 mixed-use-zoned parcels — High Street, West Broad, Broad Street, Lane/Indianola — needs to re-run their pro formas. Parking minimum removal and height bonuses can materially shift deal viability. The window to shape the specific standards is open right now, not after adoption.
- **Source(s)**: [Columbus Growth Strategy Adopted — City of Columbus GovDelivery](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/OHCCC/bulletins/40649b5), [WOSU: City Council considers citywide land use map](https://www.wosu.org/politics-government/2026-01-08/columbus-city-council-to-consider-citywide-land-use-map-despite-concerns-from-area-commissions), [NBC4: Columbus passes first zoning changes in decades](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/columbus-passes-first-zoning-code-changes-in-decades-what-to-know/), [10TV: City Council greenlights Zone In Phase 2](https://www.10tv.com/article/news/local/columbus-city-council-approves-phase-two-zone-in-initiative/530-777ab85d-46d5-4974-89fa-ad20ee8f27fd), [Zone In Columbus hub](https://zone-in-columbus.hub.arcgis.com/), [KJK Law: Reshaping the Skyline](https://kjk.com/2024/04/26/reshaping-the-skyline-the-modernization-of-the-city-of-columbus-zoning-code/), [WOSU: Rift between city and neighborhoods](https://www.wosu.org/politics-government/2025-12-01/rift-appears-between-city-and-neighborhoods-over-columbus-growth-plan)

### 2. LinkUS West Broad BRT: 9-Month TOD Planning Process Is Live

- **What**: COTA and the City of Columbus launched a 9-month transit-oriented development planning process (April–December 2026) for the 9.3-mile West Broad BRT corridor, the first major transit corridor funded by the November 2024 voter-approved LinkUS sales tax levy. A public input session was held April 10, 2026. The plan calls for dense, walkable, mixed-use communities within a 10-minute walk of new transit stops.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: TOD corridors historically compress station-area land pricing windows fast. Columbus operators and investors who want to position ahead of BRT buildout have months, not years, before the planning framework locks in station-area designations and zoning overlays. This corridor also directly intersects Zone In's mixed-use zones and the Hilltop RISE project (see brief #3).
- **Source(s)**: [nationaltoday.com: Columbus residents weigh in on LinkUS West Broad](https://nationaltoday.com/us/oh/columbus/news/2026/04/10/columbus-residents-weigh-in-on-linkus-west-broad-corridor-development/), [linkuscolumbus.com](https://linkuscolumbus.com), [MORPC — LinkUS TOD coverage]

### 3. Hilltop RISE Begins Spring Renovation on West Broad Historic Buildings

- **What**: Nonprofit Hilltop RISE Economic Community Development Corp. is starting spring 2026 renovations on two historic buildings at 3085–3093 and 3095–3099 W. Broad St. in the Westgate neighborhood of Hilltop, creating 10 affordable apartments (rents capped at 80% AMI) and approximately 6,000 sq ft of ground-floor commercial space. The Columbus City Council approved a $1.2M grant for the purchase in fall 2025; the nonprofit closed on the buildings in December and has committed to long-term affordable rents. Architecture by Arch City Development Consultants.
- **Why it matters (CRE News frame)**: A rare affordable rehab project directly on the future West Broad BRT corridor — it's the on-the-ground implementation of the transit-oriented affordable development argument that's more theory than practice most places. For policy watchers and operators tracking the Hilltop submarket, this is a signal about who's getting there first.

### 6. CMHA's AspireCOLUMBUS: $29M Affordable Complex at Former United Way HQ, Summer Groundbreaking

- **What**: Columbus Metropolitan Housing Authority plans to break ground this summer at 360 S. Third St. — the former United Way of Central Ohio headquarters — on AspireCOLUMBUS, an 82-unit affordable housing complex with 17 studio, 33 one-bedroom, 23 two-bedroom, and 9 three-bedroom units. Thirty-one units (38%) will be rent-subsidized via HCV; total project cost is ~$29M, including $17.5M in 9% LIHTC from OHFA.

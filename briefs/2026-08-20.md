### LinkedIn

Columbus's most complex downtown construction project in recent years is nearly done.

The Merchant Building — a 32-story tower on the former parking lot behind North Market — is on schedule to complete this fall, according to NBC4 and trade reporting from Multifamily Dive and Construction Dive. When it delivers, it will add:

- 174 apartments
- 206 hotel rooms (luxury hotel with a private members' social club)
- 68,000 square feet of Class A office space
- 15,000 square feet of new North Market — eight to nine vendor stalls, a bar, an event space, and a redesigned outdoor plaza

That's an unusual amount of use-mix in a single building for Columbus. The residential piece alone matters more right now than it would have a year ago: the metro's multifamily pipeline is running 40–50% below 2025 delivery volumes, so new downtown units are adding to a thinner pool.

The North Market piece is worth watching separately. The market named its first permanent merchant for the new space last spring: Caroline's, a pop-up restaurant from chef Justin Wotring, will move from its temporary setup into a permanent stall in the new building. The expansion bar and event space suggest the market is building for both daily traffic and private events.

A note on the project's history: when construction crews started clearing the old parking lot in 2023, they found it had been built over a 19th-century cemetery. Not everything had been relocated when the cemetery closed. The delay to handle that properly is part of why this project has taken three years to get here.

City of Columbus committed $4.2M to redo the streets around the tower before it opens — a sign the city is treating the block as a complete package, not just a private development.

Hotel brand, office tenants, and residential leasing details have not been publicly confirmed as of this writing.

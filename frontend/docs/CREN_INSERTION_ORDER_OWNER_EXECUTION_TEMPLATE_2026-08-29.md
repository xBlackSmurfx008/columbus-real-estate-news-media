## 4. Advertiser Materials

- Proof of rights to use all submitted assets.
- Proof for objective claims, including price, rate, availability, rent, fee, concession, credential, award, guarantee, testimonial, ranking, performance, return, or comparison claims.
- Fair-housing, lending, insurance, legal, securities, brokerage, or professional-license disclosures where relevant.

## 5. Review and Approval

CREN may review, reject, pause, remove, relabel, or require edits to any placement for:

- Unsupported objective claims.
- Fair-housing, consumer-protection, privacy, intellectual-property, professional-licensing, advertising, or brand-safety concerns.
- Confusing sponsored/editorial presentation.
- Deceptive, discriminatory, infringing, defamatory, spam, malware, unsafe, or unlawful content.
- Landing-page changes that make the original approval inaccurate.

## 6. Editorial Independence

Advertising does not purchase:

- Newsroom coverage.
- Editorial recommendations.
- Rankings.
- Corrections outcomes.
- Source treatment.
- Market-data conclusions.
- Event programming conclusions.
- Profile verification labels beyond the paid-placement label itself.

## 7. Lead Routing

- Lead routing must be specifically disclosed to users before routing.
- This order must state whether compensation is a flat ad fee, sponsorship fee, affiliate commission, referral fee, pay-per-lead fee, pay-per-lease fee, success fee, or other compensation.
- Current launch posture: use flat sponsorship or advertising fees unless counsel approves any regulated referral, lending, insurance, brokerage, or capital-introduction model.

## 8. Reporting

CREN may report available metrics such as:

- Flight dates and proof of placement.
- Page views, sends, open proxy, clicks, CTR, profile actions, lead count, lead status, source pages, and reader issues where available.
- Methodology and limitations.
- Renewal recommendation.

CREN does not guarantee impressions, clicks, leads, applications, tours, leases, sales, closings, rankings, editorial coverage, or business results unless this order expressly states otherwise.

## 9. Cancellation, Refunds, and Make-Goods

- Advertiser-caused late assets, unsupported claims, rejected copy, landing-page issues, or policy violations do not automatically create refunds.
- CREN-caused missed placements, material label errors, or delivery failures may be eligible for a make-good or partial credit.
- Completed sponsored work is generally non-refundable unless stated otherwise in the accepted order.
- Any custom refund, make-good, category exclusivity, or cancellation term must be written in the order before acceptance.

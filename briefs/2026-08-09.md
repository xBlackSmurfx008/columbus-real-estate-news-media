## Briefs

### 1. Columbus Tops Nation in Multifamily Permitting (realtor.com / NBC4 report)
- **What**: Columbus issued 4.3 new multifamily units per 1,000 residents in 2025 — the highest rate in the country and the highest since 2019, per a June 2026 rental report by realtor.com (via NBC4). Columbus also ranks 5th nationally in total multifamily permit volume (10TV). Zone In Phase 2 now covers 43% of the city and is projected to unlock 80,000+ additional units over 10 years.
- **Why it matters**: A historic permit rate is the leading indicator for 2027–2028 supply delivery. National operators are entering (NRP Group's first market-rate Columbus project broke ground last week). The affordable gap — estimated at 53,000 units — is not shrinking at the same pace. Suburban resistance to Zone In-style reforms remains a constraint.
- **Source(s)**: [NBC4](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/columbus-leads-nation-in-multifamily-home-permitting-report-shows/) · [10TV](https://www.10tv.com/article/news/local/boomtown-ohio/columbus-ranks-5th-nationally-multifamily-housing-permits/530-9675fb62-06e9-4253-93c6-ecc37077f58e) · [CRE Daily](https://www.credaily.com/briefs/multifamily-permitting-surge-places-columbus-in-national-top-10/) · [Columbus Underground (Zone In)](https://columbusunderground.com/next-phase-of-zoning-reform-will-cover-43-of-city-bw1/) · [Hoodline (April 2026)](https://hoodline.com/2026/04/ginther-s-big-housing-push-hits-a-wall-in-columbus-suburbs/) · [Columbus CEO (Aug 4)](https://www.columbusceo.com/story/business/briefs/2026/08/04/columbus-region-leaders-unite-to-tackle-the-local-housing-crisis/91170007007/)
